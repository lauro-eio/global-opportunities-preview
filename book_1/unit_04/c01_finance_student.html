Shop Talk: How Much?

Listen and repeat these phrases for buying tools.
1. How much is it? (Asking about one item)
2. What's the price? (Asking about the cost)
3. It's ten pounds. (Stating the price of one item)
4. That's twenty dollars total. (Stating a total price)

Remember to stress the important words in questions and the number in answers.

Your Work Tools Checklist

Work in groups. Buy tools for your job.
One person is the shopkeeper, others are customers.
Customers: Choose a tool. Ask for its price. Use 'How much is it?' or 'What's the price?'
Shopkeeper: Answer. Use 'It's [number] [currency].' or 'That's [number] total.'
Say 'I'll buy it!' and 'Here's the cash.'
Swap roles.
