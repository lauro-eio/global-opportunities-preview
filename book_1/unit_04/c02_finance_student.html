Understanding Your Pay

You finish your work today. Your manager gives you money.

Manager: 'Here is your pay.'
Worker: 'Thank you!'

Look at the money.

This is cash.
This is not a bill.
Is this my pay?

Talking About Your Money

Work with a partner. You are at work. One person is the manager, one is the worker.

Manager: Give the worker their daily pay. Say 'Here is your money.'
Worker: Respond. Use 'This is...' and 'Is this...?' about the pay.

Example:
Manager: 'Here is your pay today.'
Worker: 'Oh, thank you! This is money. Is this for my work?'
