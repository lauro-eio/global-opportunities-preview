Lead-In: Your Daily Work and Pay

Teacher notes

1. Begin by asking learners about their typical workday or a short job they might do.
2. Elicit what they receive at the end of that shift or job.
3. Guide the discussion towards 'pay' and 'money'.
4. Ask about expressions of gratitude (e.g., 'thank you').
5. Connect this to the idea of receiving daily wages for work.

2. What do you say when you receive money for your work today?

MFP

Presentation: This is My Pay

Teacher notes

1. Present the target structure: 'This is + cash/price'.
2. Use a real-life scenario: A manager gives daily wages. Point to real or pictured money.
3. Elicit the meaning: Identifying physical money and cost elements.
4. Conduct concept checking questions (CCQs): 'Am I pointing to an object? (Yes)', 'Is it money? (Yes)'.
5. Model the affirmative ('This is cash.'), negative ('This is not a bill.'), and question forms ('Is this my pay?').
6. Drill pronunciation of the target structure.
7. Highlight potential errors like 'This are cash.' or 'Is this price?'.

CP

Controlled Practice: Pointing to Pay

Teacher notes

1. Set up tasks that drill the affirmative, negative, and question forms of 'This is...'.
2. Provide visual cues (e.g., flashcards, objects, or descriptions) for the learners to complete the sentences.
3. Anticipate common errors such as 'This are cash.' or 'Is this price?' and provide correction.
4. Monitor learners closely for accuracy in both form and meaning.

FP

Freer Practice: Talking About Wages

Teacher notes

1. Divide learners into pairs.
2. Assign roles: Manager and Worker.
3. Explain the scenario: The Manager gives the Worker their daily wages. The Worker responds using 'This is...' and 'Is this...?' about the pay.
4. Encourage learners to use the target language in a more natural, communicative way.
5. Circulate and monitor for fluency and appropriate use of the target language.
6. Provide delayed feedback on errors, focusing on communication first.

Sample interaction:
Manager: 'Here is your pay today.'
Worker: 'Oh, thank you! This is money. Is this for my work?'
Manager: 'Yes, this is your full pay.'
Worker: 'Great! Thank you again.'

Talking About Your Money
