Your Workplace, Your Safety

Think about your workplace. Discuss with a partner:
1. What are the top three safety rules you must follow daily?
2. Why are these rules legally important for everyone's well-being?
3. Have you ever seen a safety rule that seemed unnecessary, or one that was extremely important but often ignored?

Reporting a Workplace Issue

Read the conversation between a team leader, Alex, and a worker, Ben, about a safety concern. Pay attention to how Ben reports the issue and what Alex suggests.

Alex: Good morning, Ben. What's on your mind?
Ben: Morning, Alex. I need to bring something to your attention regarding the loading bay. I've noticed a few forklifts operating too close to pedestrian walkways, especially during peak hours. It feels like a significant hazard.
Alex: Thank you for reporting that, Ben. That's a serious concern. Have you seen any near-misses?
Ben: Not yet, thankfully, but it's only a matter of time if we don't reinforce the current safety protocol. I'm worried about someone getting injured. We also haven't checked the PPE requirements for visitors in that area for a while, I think.
Alex: You're right to flag this. We need to conduct an immediate hazard assessment of the area. I'll also ensure our team re-reads the updated safety protocol guidelines on vehicle-pedestrian separation. We may need to file an internal incident report detailing your observations and our preventative actions, especially with the upcoming regulatory inspection next month. For now, please remind anyone you see operating unsafely.
Ben: Will do, Alex. Thanks for taking it seriously.
Alex: Workplace safety is everyone's responsibility, Ben. Your vigilance is appreciated.

Workplace Safety Challenge

Work in pairs. You will role-play a scenario involving a workplace safety issue. Try to use phrases from the previous activity.

Scenario:
A new piece of equipment has been installed in your factory/warehouse/office. You (Worker) have noticed that the operating instructions are unclear, and the emergency stop button is not easily accessible. You are also concerned that the required PPE requirements (e.g., safety glasses, gloves) are not being consistently enforced when operating it.

Role A: The Worker
You have a serious concern about the safety of the new equipment. You need to report this to your supervisor. Clearly explain:
* What the hazard is.
* Why it's a problem (potential consequences).
* Suggest what actions need to be taken (e.g., reviewing safety protocol, conducting a new hazard assessment).

Role B: The Supervisor
You are the supervisor. Your worker is coming to you with a safety concern. You need to:
* Listen carefully and professionally.
* Acknowledge the seriousness of the issue.
* Ask clarifying questions.
* Explain what steps will be taken next (e.g., checking PPE requirements, reviewing safety protocol, considering an incident report or a future regulatory inspection).
* Reassure the worker that their concerns are being taken seriously.

Reviewing Our Safety Reports

Let's reflect on our discussions and reports.

1. Share: What were the most challenging parts of explaining the safety issues or responding to them?
2. Professionalism: Did you feel you used professional language effectively?
3. Improvements: What's one thing you could improve in your next safety report or discussion?
