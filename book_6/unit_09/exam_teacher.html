1. q01lexis · class 1
An employee who feels unfairly treated can initiate a formal _________ by submitting a written complaint to HR.
  • A.grievance procedure ✓
  • B.disciplinary action
  • C.mediation session
  • D.complaint form
ExplanationA 'grievance procedure' is the formal process for an employee to raise a workplace concern or complaint. While a 'complaint form' might be part of it, the 'procedure' encompasses the entire process.

  • B.HR policy
  • C.disciplinary action ✓
  • D.grievance procedure
Explanation'Disciplinary action' refers to the steps taken by an employer to address an employee's misconduct or poor performance. 'Grievance procedure' is for employee complaints, not employer enforcement.
