Tasks

Match each term to its correct definition.

  • grievance procedure — A formal, step-by-step process for employees to raise and resolve workplace complaints.
  • mediation — A process involving a neutral third party who helps resolve a dispute by facilitating communication and agreement.
  • HR policy — The official rules and guidelines set by the Human Resources department that govern employee conduct and company operations.
  • disciplinary action — Formal steps or punishment taken by an employer against an employee for misconduct or rule violations.
  • complaint form — A standardized written document used to formally submit a grievance or report a workplace issue to HR.

Materials

HR Guide: Resolving Workplace Concerns

To All Employees,

At [Company Name], we are committed to fostering a fair and respectful workplace. We understand that issues can arise, and we have clear procedures in place to ensure they are addressed effectively and professionally.

Should you encounter a workplace concern, we encourage you to first attempt an informal resolution directly with the colleague or manager involved, if appropriate and safe to do so. This often resolves minor issues quickly.

If an informal approach isn't suitable or has been unsuccessful, our formal grievance procedure is available. This process is designed to handle serious complaints fairly and confidentially. To initiate this, you will need to submit a formal complaint form to the Human Resources department. This form helps us gather all necessary details and ensures your concern is officially recorded.

Upon receipt, HR will review your submission. Depending on the nature of the issue, we may facilitate mediation between the involved parties. A neutral third-party mediator will help guide a constructive conversation aimed at reaching a mutually agreeable solution. This step is often very effective in resolving misunderstandings and finding common ground.

It’s crucial to remember that all actions taken will adhere strictly to our established HR policy. This policy outlines employee rights, responsibilities, and the framework for addressing various workplace situations. Failure to follow company guidelines or engaging in misconduct, after due investigation, may result in disciplinary action. This can range from verbal warnings to, in severe cases, termination of employment.

Our goal is always to resolve disputes amicably and maintain a positive working environment for everyone. Please consult the HR portal for full details on any of these procedures or contact your HR representative for confidential guidance.

Sincerely,
The HR Department
