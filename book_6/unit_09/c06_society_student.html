Subject: Urgent Query: Professional Certification Requirements

Dear Mr. Davies,

I am writing to you with an urgent query regarding the certification requirements for operating specialized equipment, specifically the Class C forklift, which is integral to my role in the warehouse operations.

Yesterday, during a routine safety briefing, a colleague mentioned a recent update from the regulatory body concerning mandatory refresher training for all Class C operators. While I completed my initial certification last year, I haven't received any notification about additional training or changes to the permit renewal schedule.

My records indicate my permit is valid for another six months, but I am concerned about potential non-compliance if there's new legislation I'm unaware of. I want to ensure I am fully compliant to avoid any disruption to operations or personal penalties.

Could you please clarify if there have been any recent changes that might affect my current certification status or the application form for its renewal? Specifically, I'd like to know if new training is required before my next renewal date or if we are due for a compliance audit soon.

I am prepared to undertake any necessary training immediately to maintain my professional standing and ensure continuous operational efficiency.

Thank you for your prompt attention to this matter.

Best regards,
Sarah Jenkins
Warehouse Logistics Specialist

Writing Your License Renewal Inquiry

You work for a logistics company. Your professional license for operating a specific type of machinery is due for renewal in three months. You recently heard informal rumors about significant changes to the certification requirements, including new mandatory training modules, but you haven't received official communication. You are concerned about potential delays or non-compliance if these rumors are true.

Write an email to your manager, Mr./Ms. [Manager's Last Name], to:

1. State the purpose of your email clearly.
2. Express your concern about the rumored changes to certification requirements.
3. Request clarification on any updates from the regulatory body regarding your permit renewal.
4. Ask about the process for any new mandatory training or an updated application form.
5. Reassure your manager of your commitment to maintaining full compliance.

Audience: Your direct manager.
Purpose: To proactively seek information and ensure continuous compliance.
Length: Approximately 150-200 words.

Use appropriate professional language and refer to the example email and useful phrases from the previous stages.

Improving Your Professional Correspondence

Now, exchange your email with a partner. Read your partner's email and provide constructive feedback using the questions below. Then, take time to review your own email and make improvements.

Peer Feedback Questions:
1. Clarity of Purpose: Is the main reason for the email clear from the beginning?
2. Information Request: Are the questions or requests for information clear and specific?
3. Professional Tone: Is the tone appropriate for an email to a manager? Is it respectful and proactive?
4. Key Vocabulary: Has your partner used any of the lexical tags we discussed (e.g., regulatory body, certification requirements, permit renewal, compliance audit, application form) effectively?
5. Completeness: Does the email address all five points from the writing brief?
6. Overall Impression: Is the email easy to understand and does it achieve its purpose effectively?

After receiving feedback, revise your email to improve clarity, tone, and accuracy.
