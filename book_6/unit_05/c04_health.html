Key Terms for Communication

Before we listen, let's look at some important words you'll hear. These terms are often used when we talk about sharing new rules or information at work.

Materials

Vocabulary
  • directive dissemination
  • internal communication
  • clarity
  • compliance
  • feedback mechanism
  • protocol
  • adhere
  • safeguard
  • operational continuity
Tasks

Match each term to its definition.

  • directive dissemination — The process of sharing official instructions or orders widely.
  • internal communication — Sharing information among people within the same organization.
  • clarity — The quality of being clear, easy to understand.
  • compliance — Following rules, standards, or laws.
  • feedback mechanism — A system for receiving reactions or suggestions about a product or activity.
  • protocol — A set of detailed rules for a particular situation or procedure.
  • adhere — To stick firmly to a rule, belief, or plan.
  • safeguard — To protect someone or something from harm or damage.
  • operational continuity — The ability of an organization to continue essential functions during and after a disruption.
GIST

Gist Listening: Overview of the New Health Policy

Teacher notes

1. Explain that students will listen to an HR manager discussing a new health policy.
2. Tell students to listen for the main purpose of the announcement, not specific details.
3. Play the audio (or read the script).
4. Have students answer the multiple-choice question to check their general understanding.
5. Discuss the answer as a class.

Student handout

First Listen: What's the Policy About?

Listen to an HR Manager explaining a new company health policy. Don't worry about every detail, just focus on the main point of her announcement.

Materials

Briefing on New Company Health Guidelines

Good morning everyone. Thank you for making time for this important briefing. As you know, ensuring a safe and healthy workplace is our top priority, and with the changing landscape of public health, we're rolling out a new set of health guidelines effective immediately. This policy is crucial for maintaining a healthy environment for all our staff across diverse teams.

Firstly, let's talk about directive dissemination. We need to ensure every colleague understands these updates. The full document will be shared via email and posted on the staff portal. However, I’ll summarize the key points now.

The main change involves our protocol for reporting illness and returning to work. We're moving to a symptom-based assessment, rather than a test-based one, for common seasonal illnesses. If you experience certain symptoms, you're required to stay home and notify your line manager immediately. Your manager will then follow the internal communication protocol to report your absence to HR. This is about protecting everyone.

Secondly, compliance is paramount. These aren't suggestions; they are mandatory protocols designed for collective well-being. Managers, you play a vital role in ensuring your teams adhere to these guidelines. We understand that this might require some adjustments, especially for those working shifts or in roles where presence is perceived as essential. We've introduced clearer pathways for temporary role adjustments where feasible.

Thirdly, for clarity, we've updated the sick leave request form and simplified the return-to-work checklist. Please review these documents carefully. If anything is unclear, don't hesitate to ask questions. We're also providing a dedicated helpline for specific health-related queries, accessible during business hours.

Finally, and perhaps most importantly, we want to establish a robust feedback mechanism. We recognize that new policies can raise concerns or bring up unforeseen practical challenges. There will be an anonymous suggestion box, both physical and digital, and we’re scheduling follow-up Q&A sessions next week with department leads. Your input is invaluable in refining our approach and addressing any issues that may arise. We want to hear from you.

This new policy is a collective effort to safeguard our health and maintain operational continuity. Your cooperation in understanding and implementing these guidelines is greatly appreciated. Remember, effective internal communication and strict compliance will be key to its success. Thank you.

Tasks

Listen to the HR Manager. What is the main purpose of her announcement?

The main purpose of the HR Manager's announcement is to:

  • Introduce new company products and services.
  • Inform staff about a new company-wide health policy and how it will be implemented.
  • Discuss employee vacation schedules for the coming year.
  • Recruit new staff for the HR department.
DET

Detailed Listening: Key Actions and Responsibilities

Teacher notes

1. Explain that students will listen again, this time for specific details about the policy's implementation and individual responsibilities.
2. Play the audio again.
3. Have students answer the short-text and multiple-choice questions.
4. For the open inference question, discuss student answers and provide the model answer below.
5. Check answers as a class.

Model answer for open inference: The company ensures understanding by summarizing key points, sharing documents via email and staff portal, simplifying forms, and offering a dedicated helpline for clarity. They ensure active participation and address concerns through a robust feedback mechanism, including an anonymous suggestion box, and scheduling follow-up Q&A sessions with department leads.

Student handout

Second Listen: Who Does What?

Listen again carefully. This time, focus on the details of the new health policy, specific actions required, and who is responsible for what.

After listening, consider this: How does the company try to ensure both understanding and active participation from its employees regarding the new policy? Think about the different strategies mentioned.

FBDC

Follow-Up Discussion and Language Feedback

Teacher notes

1. Open a discussion about the content of the listening and the effectiveness of the communication strategies.
2. Ask students if they think the HR manager's approach would be effective in their own workplace. Why/why not?
3. Provide feedback on any emergent language issues related to the lesson's lexical set or listening comprehension strategies.
4. Recap key vocabulary used in the lesson, focusing on terms like internal communication, directive dissemination, compliance, feedback mechanism.
5. Discuss how the company tried to ensure clarity.
