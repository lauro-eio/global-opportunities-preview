Targeted Clarification: Causative & Necessity

Teacher notes

1. Review answers from T1, highlighting common errors without explicitly naming students.
2. Focus on the two structures: Causative Passive (have/get something done) and Gerundial Necessity (need + -ing).
3. Use the provided meaning and concept check questions to clarify:
* Meaning: 'Arranging for professional public policy inspections, healthcare system audits, or confirming that safety equipment or bioethics procedures require an upgrade.'
* CCQ for Causative: Ask, 'Am I performing the safety audit or update myself?' (Expected student answer: 'No, a professional or external system is doing it'). Emphasize the 'getting someone else to do it' aspect.
* CCQ for Gerundial: Ask, 'Does 'the policy needs updating' mean someone must update the policy?' (Expected student answer: 'Yes').
4. Display the correct forms clearly (Subject + have/get + Object + Past Participle / Subject + need(s) + Verb+ing).
5. Conduct a quick practice activity using the tasks below.

Student handout

Ensuring Smooth Operations: Policy & Procedure

We've identified some areas where we can refine our use of language to more precisely describe operational necessities and actions outsourced to others. Let's review two key structures:

1. Causative Passive (have/get something done):
This structure is used when we arrange for someone else to do something for us, rather than doing it ourselves. The focus is on the result or the action being performed by an external agent.

* Form: Subject + have/get + Object + Past Participle
* Example: 'We had the new health sensors installed by a certified technician.' (We didn't install them ourselves).

2. Gerundial Operational Necessities (need + -ing):
This structure expresses that something requires action or attention. It implies a passive meaning ('needs to be done') without explicitly stating the agent.

* Form: Subject + need(s) + Verb+ing
* Example: 'The data privacy protocols need reviewing before deployment.' (They need to be reviewed).

Now, let's practice applying these structures correctly in various workplace scenarios.

T2

Integrative Task: Proposing Health Tech Implementation

Teacher notes

1. Introduce the scenario: a company meeting to discuss the implementation of new health technology.
2. Instruct students to work in pairs or small groups.
3. Emphasize the need to use the target structures (Causative Passive, Gerundial Necessity) naturally within their discussion and proposed solutions.
4. Circulate, listen for correct usage, and provide support. Note down excellent examples and areas for further review.
5. After discussion, have groups share their main points, focusing on the application of the target language.
6. For the countable task, remind students to use one of the target structures. Sample correct responses for the task:
* 'We need to have our security systems thoroughly audited by an independent firm.'
* 'Our system needs updating to comply with the latest data protection regulations.'

Strategic Implementation Plan: Health Tech

Your organization is holding a follow-up meeting to discuss the implementation of a new wearable health technology system for employees. You are part of the project team, responsible for outlining the next steps and addressing concerns.

Work in small groups. Imagine you are presenting to management and the legal department. Discuss the following points, making sure to use the structures we reviewed today (Causative Passive and Gerundial Operational Necessities) where appropriate:

1. Data Security & Privacy: What actions need to be taken to ensure employee data is protected? Who will perform these actions, or how will they be arranged?
2. Training & Adoption: How will employees be trained on the new technology? What needs to be done to encourage widespread adoption?
3. Policy & Compliance: What existing company policies (e.g., health & safety, bioethics) might need reviewing or updating? What new policies might need implementing?
4. Hardware Maintenance: How will the wearable devices be maintained and repaired? Will the company have them serviced internally or by an external provider?

Prepare to share your key recommendations and justifications with the class, highlighting how you will manage these operational necessities.
