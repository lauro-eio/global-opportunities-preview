Workplace Wellbeing: Initial Thoughts

Modern work environments can be very demanding, often leading to stress and even burnout. Effective strategies are crucial to support employees.

Discuss with a partner or small group:
1. What specific measures do you think employers can take to prevent workplace stress from escalating into burnout among staff in practical, hands-on roles?
2. What are the most common challenges in implementing these measures in your experience?

TCH

Teach: Understanding Professional Register

Teacher notes

Review the answers from T1, highlighting the correct professional phrases.
Draw attention to the difference in register between the correct answers and the incorrect options.
Explain the meaning and nuance of each target exponent, emphasizing why they are appropriate for formal workplace communication (e.g., policy, respect, clarity).
Use CCQs (Concept Checking Questions) for each phrase: "Is 'ensure employee wellbeing' something you say to your friend or your boss?" "Does 'address concerns' mean to ignore them or actively deal with them?" "Is 'implement' more formal than 'start'?"
Point out the phonological features: "Notice the emphasis on key words like employee, safety, wellness, support. The falling intonation helps convey seriousness and professionalism."
Guide students through the matching task to consolidate understanding of professional vs. informal.

We will now look at how the professional phrases you chose are more suitable and what makes them effective in a workplace setting.

Teacher notes

Divide students into small groups or pairs.
Present the scenario and task clearly.
Emphasize the need to use the professional vocabulary learned (the target exponents) naturally within their discussion.
Monitor carefully, noting down successful uses and areas for improvement in register and appropriacy.
Provide feedback at the end, focusing on the effective use of professional language.
Model Answer Hint (for teacher reference, not for students): Students should create a plan that logically uses phrases like 'We need to ensure employee wellbeing by proactively identifying stressors.' 'It's crucial to address health and safety concerns related to shift patterns.' 'Our proposal is to implement wellness programs focusing on resilience.' 'Employees should be encouraged to seek medical advice early.' 'And importantly, make sure everyone knows how to access support services.'

Student handout

Developing Workplace Wellbeing Strategies

Imagine you are part of a small team responsible for improving staff well-being in a demanding workplace, such as a factory, logistics hub, or service industry setting. Management has asked you to propose a few key strategies to prevent stress and burnout.

Work with your partner or group to formulate a brief plan (3-4 key points). In your discussion and when presenting your ideas, make sure to use the professional phrases we have practised:

* ensure employee wellbeing
* address health and safety concerns
* implement wellness programs
* seek medical advice
* access support services

Think about practical, actionable steps that respect the dignity of working-class learners and the realities of their jobs.
