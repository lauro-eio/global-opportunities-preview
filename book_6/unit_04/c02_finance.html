First Look: Q3 Budget Review

Read the memo below quickly. Don't worry about every detail, just try to understand the main reason it was sent.

Materials

Memo: Q3 Performance Review & Cost-Saving Initiatives

To: All Department Heads
From: Eleanor Vance, Finance Director
Date: October 25th
Subject: Q3 Performance Review & Upcoming Cost-Saving Initiatives

Dear Department Heads,

I am writing to provide an overview of our financial performance for the third fiscal quarter and outline critical areas requiring immediate attention. Overall, the company continues to demonstrate resilience, yet certain departmental expenditures have shown concerning variance against our initial budget allocations. While we managed to post a significant profit globally, largely due to strong international sales, we must address internal spending more rigorously.

Specifically, the operational overhead in several departments has significantly impacted our overall return on investment (ROI). The surge in unexpected maintenance costs was particularly notable. Additionally, raw material costs have seen an upward trend, directly affecting our production budget. We must act decisively to maintain our projected growth figures and ensure long-term stability.

What is clear from our analysis is the urgent need to re-evaluate our procurement processes. What we are primarily focused on now is identifying sustainable strategies for operational efficiency. To that end, effective immediately, all non-critical expenditures will require explicit approval from the Finance Department. We believe that a more stringent approach will help us mitigate potential risks as market sentiment remains cautious.

We will be scheduling individual meetings with each department over the next two weeks to discuss specific areas where cost-cutting measures can be implemented without compromising service delivery or employee well-being. What we aim to achieve is a lean yet robust operational model.

Your cooperation and proactive engagement in these initiatives are paramount to our collective success. Thank you for your understanding and commitment.

Sincerely,
Eleanor Vance
Finance Director

Guided Discovery: Cleft Sentences & Financial Pragmatics

Teacher notes

1. Direct learners back to the memo to identify specific linguistic features.
2. For cleft sentences, ask learners to find sentences starting with 'What...' and discuss their function in emphasizing a point.
3. Explain the form and meaning of cleft sentences ('What + clause + is/was + emphasized element') for isolating and stressing critical information in formal contexts.
4. For financial vocabulary, guide learners to match the terms to their formal definitions/functions.
5. Emphasize the C2 register and pragmatic function of these phrases in professional settings.

FP

Freer Practice: Presenting Departmental Financial Needs

Teacher notes

1. Divide learners into pairs or small groups.
2. Ask them to brainstorm a specific departmental scenario (e.g., requesting a budget increase for a new tool, proposing a cost-saving measure, explaining unexpected expenditure).
3. Instruct them to draft a short statement (3-5 sentences) addressing the Finance Director, Eleanor Vance.
4. The statement must incorporate at least one cleft sentence to emphasize a key financial point and at least two pieces of professional financial vocabulary.
5. Emphasize the C2 register: diplomatic, professional, clear, and persuasive.
6. Circulate and provide support, focusing on accurate and appropriate use of target structures and vocabulary.
7. Learners can then share their statements with the class for peer feedback and discussion.

Sample Answer (for teacher reference): 'Good morning, Eleanor. Following the Q3 review, what we have identified is a critical need for investment in new software to improve our team's operational efficiency. While our department has maintained its budget allocations this fiscal quarter, we project a significant increase in return on investment (ROI) if we upgrade our systems, ultimately supporting broader cost-cutting initiatives across the company. We believe what is essential for future productivity is this strategic investment.'

Advocating for Your Department's Budget

Imagine you are a Department Head. You need to prepare a brief statement (3-5 sentences) for Eleanor Vance, the Finance Director, either explaining a critical budget need or proposing a cost-saving initiative for your department. Your goal is to be clear, professional, and diplomatic, yet persuasive.

In your statement:
1. Use at least one cleft sentence to emphasize a key financial point (e.g., 'What is clear is...', 'What we need is...').
2. Incorporate at least two pieces of professional financial vocabulary we discussed today (e.g., fiscal quarter, ROI, operational efficiency, cost-cutting, projected growth figures).

Prepare your statement and be ready to share it with the class.
