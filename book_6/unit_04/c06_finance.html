Lead-In: Market Shifts and Investment Decisions

Teacher notes

1. Begin by asking students about recent developments in their industries.
2. Guide the discussion to new technologies or shifts in consumer behavior.
3. Ask students to consider how these changes might impact their companies.
4. Facilitate a brief discussion on how companies generally decide where to invest next.

Student handout

New Trends, New Investments

Think about your industry. What new technologies or shifts in consumer behavior have emerged recently?

1. How have these developments impacted your company or sector?
2. What are the potential opportunities or challenges that these changes present?
3. In your experience, how do companies typically decide where to invest their resources to respond to such trends? What factors do they consider?

MOD

Model Analysis: Internal Report on Market Trends

Teacher notes

1. Instruct students to read the internal memo quickly to understand its overall purpose.
2. Set Task 1 (main purpose) as a gist-reading activity.
3. For Tasks 2 and 3, ask students to re-read more carefully, focusing on specific details and the identified target language.
4. Review answers as a class, discussing how the memo effectively conveys its message and uses professional language.

Student handout

Reading an Investment Analysis

Read the internal memo below from an analyst to a manager regarding recent market trends and investment considerations. Pay attention to how the information is presented and the language used.

Materials

Internal Memo: Q3 Market Trends & Strategic Investment Review

TO: Department Head, Strategic Initiatives
FROM: Lead Analyst, Market Research
DATE: October 26, 20XX
SUBJECT: Analysis of Q3 Market Trends & Investment Implications

This memo outlines key market trends observed in Q3 and provides an initial assessment of their implications for our company's investment strategy. Our market analysis reveals significant shifts in consumer behavior, particularly a growing preference for sustainable products and services, driven by increased environmental awareness and regulatory changes across the sector.

Key sector trends indicate a robust growth potential in renewable energy and green logistics solutions. Consumer spending data suggests a steady increase in demand for suppliers demonstrating clear sustainability commitments. Conversely, traditional energy sectors face increasing pressure from evolving legislation and public sentiment, impacting their long-term viability and potential ROI for conventional investments.

Considering these developments, our preliminary recommendation is to re-evaluate existing capital expenditure plans. A strategic pivot towards green technologies and sustainable infrastructure could significantly enhance our competitive position and secure future growth potential. This would involve allocating a greater portion of our budget to R&D in eco-friendly alternatives and investing in logistics partners with certified sustainable practices. This shift aligns with both market demand and our corporate responsibility goals.

Further detailed analysis will follow, including a comprehensive financial projection for these proposed adjustments to our investment strategy. We believe a proactive approach will be crucial for maximizing long-term ROI and maintaining leadership in an evolving market.

Tasks

What is the main purpose of this memo?

According to the memo, what is driving the preference for sustainable products?

  • Lower production costs
  • Increased environmental awareness and regulatory changes
  • Aggressive marketing campaigns by green companies
  • New technological breakthroughs

Identify two types of investments the memo suggests could enhance competitive position and secure future growth potential.

PREP

Preparation: Planning Your Investment Update

Teacher notes

1. Ask students to brainstorm a market trend or consumer shift relevant to their current work or a familiar industry.
2. Guide them to consider its potential impact on a company's investment decisions.
3. Instruct students to use the 'Report Structure' column bank to outline their own short report or memo.
4. Then, have them review the 'Useful Phrases for Investment Discussions' and identify phrases they might incorporate into their writing.
5. Encourage students to think about how they will use the target lexis effectively.

FP

Freer Practice: Drafting an Investment Memo

Teacher notes

1. Clearly explain the writing task to the students, ensuring they understand the audience, purpose, and length.
2. Encourage students to refer to the model memo and the planning/phrases from the PREP stage.
3. Circulate and monitor, providing support as needed, focusing on content and the use of target lexis.
4. Collect the memos at the end of the writing time.

Sample Model Answer (for teacher reference):

TO: Operations Director
FROM: Logistics Analyst
DATE: December 1, 20XX
SUBJECT: Analysis of E-commerce Logistics Trends & Capital Expenditure Proposal

This memo details recent sector trends in e-commerce logistics and proposes adjustments to our investment strategy. Our market analysis shows a clear shift towards localized fulfillment centers and autonomous delivery systems, driven by consumer demand for faster delivery and lower environmental impact.

There is significant growth potential in automating warehouse operations and establishing micro-distribution hubs. This would involve capital expenditure in robotic sorting technology and electric delivery vehicles to maximize ROI over the next five years. Failing to adapt could lead to reduced market share. We recommend a phased approach, starting with a pilot program in our busiest region.

Implementing these changes proactively will align our investment strategy with evolving market expectations and secure our position as a leader in efficient, sustainable logistics.

Student handout

Your Company's Next Move

Imagine you are an analyst in your company. Your manager has asked you to draft a short internal memo (approximately 150-200 words) summarizing a recent market analysis regarding a new technology or consumer shift affecting your industry. Based on this, you need to propose an investment strategy or discuss potential capital expenditure to capitalize on growth potential in specific sector trends.

Your memo should:

- Audience: Your manager or department head.
- Purpose: To inform them about relevant market trends and recommend a strategic direction.
- Key Points: Briefly describe the market trend, explain its implications, and propose a specific investment or strategic adjustment.
- Language: Use professional language and incorporate relevant phrases and vocabulary, including terms like market analysis, investment strategy, capital expenditure, growth potential, ROI, and sector trends.

FBDC

Feedback & Correction: Refining Your Report

Teacher notes

1. Pair students up and instruct them to exchange their memos for peer feedback.
2. Provide specific instructions for peer review: first, focus on the content (clarity of trend description, relevance of recommendations, persuasiveness).
3. Then, ask peers to check for appropriate use of target lexis and professional register.
4. Facilitate a brief class discussion on common strengths and areas for improvement based on peer feedback.
5. Collect the memos for final teacher feedback, focusing on areas for language accuracy and further development.

Student handout

Reviewing Your Analysis

Now, you will have the opportunity to receive and give feedback on your memo.

1. Peer Feedback (Content Focus): Exchange your memo with a partner. Read your partner's memo and consider the following:
- Is the market trend clearly described?
- Are the implications for the company logical and well-explained?
- Are the recommendations or investment proposals clear and actionable?
- Does the memo fulfill its purpose of informing the manager and proposing a direction?

2. Peer Feedback (Language Focus): Now, re-read your partner's memo, specifically looking at the language:
- Is the language professional and appropriate for an internal business memo?
- Are the target vocabulary items (e.g., market analysis, investment strategy, ROI) used accurately and effectively?
- Are there any areas where the wording could be clearer or more concise?

3. Self-Correction: Use your partner's feedback to revise and improve your own memo. Focus on making both the content and the language as strong as possible.
