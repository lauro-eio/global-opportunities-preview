Unpacking a Legal Review Email

Read the following internal email from a Project Lead to the Legal Department regarding an upcoming international service contract. Pay attention to how the key legal considerations are communicated.

Materials

Internal Memo: Review of 'Project Nexus' International Contract

Subject: Urgent Review Required for 'Project Nexus' - International Service Contract

Dear Legal Team,

I hope this email finds you well.

We are currently in the final stages of negotiating the service contract for 'Project Nexus' with our new client, Alpha Solutions, based in Dubai. Before we proceed to sign, I require your expert review and advice on several critical legal aspects.

Firstly, we need absolute clarity on the governing law of this contract. Given Alpha Solutions' location, we must decide whether to stipulate UAE law, German law (our base), or a mutually agreed-upon third-party international law framework. This decision will significantly impact any potential dispute resolution processes.

Secondly, a robust jurisdiction clause is paramount. Where would any legal proceedings or arbitration take place? We need to ensure that the chosen jurisdiction is practical and fair for both parties, minimizing operational disruption in the event of a disagreement.

Thirdly, I need your confirmation on the full compliance of this contractual agreement with all relevant local and international regulations. We must ensure there are no unforeseen liabilities or conflicts with either UAE or German commercial laws, especially concerning data protection and service delivery standards.

Finally, could you advise on the most effective dispute resolution mechanism? While we hope for a smooth partnership, preparing for contingencies is essential. Should we prioritize arbitration, mediation, or direct legal action in a specific court?

Your prompt attention to these points would be greatly appreciated. Please let me know if you require any further documentation or information. We aim to finalize this by the end of the month.

Best regards,

Markus Schmidt
Project Lead, Global Operations

Your Turn: Writing a Legal Review Memo

Scenario: You are a Procurement Manager for a manufacturing company based in Mexico. Your company is preparing to sign a new supply contract with a client based in South Korea for specialized raw materials. You need to send an internal email to your Head of Legal to outline the crucial legal considerations for this contract.

Your Task: Write an email (approximately 200-250 words) to your Head of Legal.

Purpose: To clearly articulate the essential legal points that require careful review before signing the international supply contract.
Audience: Your Head of Legal / Senior Legal Counsel.

Key points to include:
1. The importance of establishing a clear governing law for the contract (e.g., Mexican law, South Korean law, or a neutral option).
2. Which country's jurisdiction clause would be most practical and fair for potential disputes.
3. Consideration of appropriate dispute resolution mechanisms (e.g., arbitration, mediation).
4. Ensuring complete compliance with both national (Mexican and South Korean) and international law pertinent to supply chains.
5. Any specific terms of the contractual agreement (e.g., delivery terms, payment conditions) that might have legal implications and need extra attention.

Refining Your Legal Communication

Part 1: Peer Feedback
Swap your email with a partner. Read your partner's email carefully and consider the following questions. Provide constructive feedback:

1. Clarity and Purpose: Is the main purpose of the email immediately clear? Does it effectively communicate the need for a legal review?
2. Content Coverage: Does the email address all five key points outlined in the task brief? Are there any missing details or areas that need more explanation?
3. Use of Target Language: Were the specific legal terms (jurisdiction clause, governing law, international law, compliance, contractual agreement, dispute resolution) used appropriately and accurately?
4. Professional Register: Is the email's tone professional and suitable for communicating with a Head of Legal?
5. Overall Impact: What is the strongest aspect of the email? What is one specific area for improvement?

Part 2: Revision
Based on the feedback you received from your partner, take some time to review and revise your own email. Focus on making it as clear, comprehensive, and professionally written as possible.
