Speaking Up Professionally

Imagine a scenario at your workplace: a colleague makes a comment about someone's accent or national origin that is clearly inappropriate and makes others uncomfortable.

1. How would you feel hearing such a remark?
2. What are the immediate challenges in deciding how to respond?
3. What kind of impact could such a comment have on workplace morale and productivity?
4. What steps could a professional take to address this, ensuring workplace dignity and inclusion are upheld, without escalating unnecessarily?

Drafting an Inclusive Response

Read the draft internal memo from Human Resources. For each numbered blank, choose the option that best conveys a professional, strategic, and inclusive approach to addressing workplace issues.

Materials

Internal Memo: Reinforcing Workplace Standards

Subject: Reinforcing Our Commitment to an Inclusive Workplace Environment

Dear Team,

Following recent discussions and feedback, we wish to reiterate our steadfast commitment to fostering a truly inclusive workplace. Ensuring a respectful environment where every individual feels valued is paramount to our collective success.

It has come to our attention that certain interactions may not have always upheld these standards. To proactively address this, we propose the following strategic actions:

1. We aim to [ ] on addressing the issue of insensitive comments and discriminatory practices. This involves not only reactive measures but also fostering a culture of proactive cultural sensitivity.

2. We plan to [ ] our inclusion policies, ensuring they are not only comprehensive but also effectively communicated across all departments. This is vital to preventing future incidents and promoting equal opportunity.

3. Our leadership is committed to [ ] to diversity within our organizational structure. This means ensuring that policies against prejudice and discriminatory practices are visible and actively supported.

4. To enhance efficiency in addressing concerns, we will [ ] the process for reporting and resolving grievances. Clear, accessible pathways are essential for building trust.

5. Finally, we will [ ] to support affected personnel and educate staff on workplace inclusion. This includes training programs and dedicated support services.

Your cooperation in upholding these standards is crucial. We value your contributions to a respectful workplace.

Sincerely,

The Human Resources Department

Mastering Formal Responses

These phrases are used in professional settings to discuss strategic actions, showing purposeful planning and a high level of commitment. They help to convey conviction and clarity in important communications.

Now, let's look at more precise and impactful ways to phrase your responses to workplace challenges.

Vocabulary

  • broaden the scope of our inclusion policies
  • ensure a visible commitment to diversity
  • streamline the grievance resolution process
  • allocate resources to support affected personnel

Connect informal language with more formal, strategic phrasing.

  • Get things moving on the problem. — Gain traction on addressing the issue.
  • Make our policies bigger. — Broaden the scope of our inclusion policies.
  • Show everyone we care about diversity. — Ensure a visible commitment to diversity.
  • Make the complaint process quicker. — Streamline the grievance resolution process.
  • Give money/help to people who need it. — Allocate resources to support affected personnel.

Proposing Solutions

You are a team leader in a department where recent inappropriate comments about national origin have created tension. Your manager has asked you to prepare a brief proposal (3-5 sentences) to present to the HR department and senior leadership, outlining strategic actions to restore a positive and inclusive work environment.

Working with a partner, discuss and draft your proposal. Make sure to:

* Identify the core problems clearly.
* Propose concrete, strategic actions.
* Use at least two to three of the strategic phrases you learned in the previous stage.
* Maintain a professional and solution-oriented tone.
* Practice delivering your proposal with conviction, paying attention to stress and intonation.
