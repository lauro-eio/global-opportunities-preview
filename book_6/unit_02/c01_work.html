Lead-In: Discussing Strategic Shifts

Teacher notes

1. Greet students and set the context for the lesson.
2. Explain the scenario: a major company strategy shift.
3. Encourage pair discussion on the immediate questions students would have about their roles.
4. Nominate pairs to share their questions with the class.
5. Write down a few key questions on the board to refer to later.

Student handout

Company Changes & Your Role

Imagine your CEO has just announced a major shift in company strategy. This could change how your company operates and even what you do day-to-day.

Discuss with a partner:
1. What is one immediate question you would have about how this new strategy might affect your daily tasks?
2. How important is it for a company to clearly explain these changes to its employees?
3. What kind of information would you want to know first?

PTV

Pre-Teaching Vocabulary: Corporate Strategy Terms

Teacher notes

1. Introduce the vocabulary words related to corporate strategy.
2. Elicit or provide definitions, checking for comprehension.
3. Ask students to work individually or in pairs to match the terms to their definitions.
4. Review answers as a class, clarifying any misunderstandings.

Tasks

Match each term to its definition.

  • strategic pivot — A significant change in a company's business strategy, often in response to market changes or new opportunities.
  • corporate mandate — An official instruction or order from a company's leadership that must be followed by all employees or departments.
  • operational shift — A change in the way a company's daily activities, processes, or production methods are carried out.
  • impact assessment — An evaluation of the potential effects or consequences that a particular action, change, or project will have.
GIST

Gist Reading: Understanding the Core Message

Teacher notes

1. Explain that students will read a company-wide announcement about a new strategic direction.
2. Instruct students to read the memo quickly to get the main idea, without focusing on every detail.
3. Tell them to answer the multiple-choice question based on their overall understanding.
4. Set a short time limit for skimming (e.g., 2-3 minutes).
5. Check the answer as a class and briefly discuss the main purpose of the memo.

Student handout

Materials

Company-Wide Announcement: Embracing a New Strategic Direction

Dear Team,

I hope this message finds you well. Today, I am writing to you about an exciting and essential development for our company's future. After extensive review and consultation, we are announcing a significant strategic pivot towards enhanced customer-centricity and digital integration. This is not merely an adjustment; it is a fundamental re-alignment of our priorities to ensure long-term growth and market leadership.

This corporate mandate will see us channel greater resources into understanding our customers' evolving needs, streamlining their digital experience, and empowering our frontline teams with cutting-edge tools. We recognize that in today's dynamic market, staying ahead means constantly innovating and adapting.

The immediate operational shift will involve several key changes:
1. Restructuring of Customer Support: A new digital-first approach for faster, more personalized service.
2. Investment in Training: Comprehensive upskilling programs for all employees, especially in digital literacy and data analysis.
3. Cross-Functional Collaboration: New project teams will be formed to drive innovation across departments.

We understand that a change of this magnitude requires careful consideration and an honest impact assessment for each of you. Your department heads and team leaders will soon hold dedicated sessions to discuss the specifics of these changes and how they will shape your roles and responsibilities. We are committed to supporting every employee through this transition, ensuring you have the resources and clarity needed to succeed.

This strategic pivot represents our collective commitment to excellence and a brighter future. We believe that by working together, we can embrace these changes and emerge stronger, more agile, and more aligned with our customer's needs than ever before.

Thank you for your dedication and adaptability.

Sincerely,

[CEO Name]

Tasks

What is the main purpose of this company announcement?

  • To announce new hiring for customer support roles.
  • To inform employees about a major new company strategy and its upcoming implications.
  • To celebrate recent financial successes and company growth.
  • To introduce a new CEO to the entire staff.
DET

Detailed Reading: Analyzing the Implications

Teacher notes

1. Explain that students will now read the memo more carefully to understand the details and their implications.
2. Instruct students to complete the short answer questions based on specific factual details in the text.
3. Then, ask students to discuss the numbered inference questions in pairs or small groups.
4. Circulate, monitor, and provide support. Note down any common language issues for brief feedback later.
5. Discuss answers to short answer questions as a class.
6. Elicit responses for the discussion questions, encouraging students to justify their answers from the text.

Student handout

Deep Dive: What It Means for You

Now, read the company announcement again, but this time, read it more carefully. Pay attention to the specific details of the new strategy and what they might mean for different areas of the company and for your own role.

Part 1: Factual Details
Answer the following questions with a short phrase or sentence from the memo.

Part 2: Implications and Discussion
Discuss these questions with a partner. Think about what this new strategy might mean for employees.
1. How does the company plan to support employees through this transition?
2. What kind of new skills might employees need to develop due to this operational shift?
3. If you were a department head, what would be your first step after reading this memo?
4. How do you think this strategic pivot could ultimately benefit the company and its customers?

FBDC

Feedback & Discussion: Reflecting on the Strategy

Teacher notes

1. Lead a whole-class discussion, starting with content feedback on the detailed reading questions.
2. Encourage students to share their opinions and interpretations from the discussion questions in DET.
3. Provide brief, constructive language feedback, focusing on useful vocabulary or common errors related to expressing opinions or discussing corporate changes. (No explicit grammar teaching).
4. Conclude by linking back to the initial lead-in questions and seeing if students' perspectives have changed after reading the announcement.

Student handout

Discussing the Future

Let's share our thoughts on the company's new strategic direction.

1. What do you think are the biggest challenges the company will face during this transition?
2. How important is clear communication from leadership during a major company change?
3. Based on the memo, what makes you feel optimistic or concerned about the future of the company and your role?
4. What can employees do to best adapt to and support a strategic pivot like this?
