Connecting Our Work

Think about your job or a previous job. Has your team ever set new goals or targets? How did those goals fit in with what the whole company was trying to achieve? Were they clearly connected?

Discuss with a partner:
1. Describe a time your team set new goals. What were they?
2. How did those goals relate to the company's bigger picture or strategy?
3. Was it easy or difficult to see the connection? Why?
4. What happened when your team's goals were well-aligned with the company, or not?

Tasks

Match the key terms used in corporate strategy meetings with their definitions.

Match each term to its definition.

  • strategic objectives — Broad, long-term goals that define what an organization wants to achieve.
  • key performance indicators — Measurable values that show how effectively a company is achieving its business objectives.
  • resource allocation — The process of assigning available resources (like time, money, people) to different projects or departments.
  • cross-functional collaboration — When different departments or teams work together to achieve a common goal.

First Listen: The Meeting's Aim

You will hear a segment of a team meeting. Listen once to understand the main idea. What is the primary purpose of this meeting?

Materials

Quarterly Strategy Alignment Meeting

Sarah (Head of Marketing): Good morning, everyone. Thanks for joining. As we kick off Q2, our main goal today is to ensure our departmental efforts are fully aligned with the company's overarching strategic objectives for the next quarter. Specifically, we need to finalize our approach to 'Market Penetration in Sector B' and 'Enhancing Customer Loyalty'.

David (Head of Sales): Absolutely, Sarah. From Sales, we're seeing strong potential in Sector B, but our current lead generation isn't quite hitting the mark for our expansion targets. We've been tracking our key performance indicators (KPIs) and while conversion rates are good, the initial volume needs a boost.

Maria (CEO): Exactly, David. Our company-wide target is a 15% increase in market share in Sector B by the end of Q2. Marketing and Sales must work hand-in-hand here. Sarah, what are your immediate thoughts on how Marketing can support David's team more effectively to drive that initial volume?

Sarah (Head of Marketing): We've identified a need for increased digital ad spend and targeted content campaigns for Sector B. However, this impacts our existing resource allocation for other initiatives. We need to discuss how much budget and team hours we can realistically shift without compromising our customer loyalty program.

David (Head of Sales): From our side, we're prepared to dedicate additional sales reps to follow up on those new leads, but the quality of leads from Marketing will be crucial. Poor quality leads waste valuable sales time.

Maria (CEO): This is precisely why cross-functional collaboration is paramount. Sarah, David, I need a clear plan by end of day for how your teams will share data, synchronize campaigns, and adjust resource allocation to meet that 15% market share growth. We can’t afford any disconnects between lead generation and follow-up. Our strategic objectives depend on it.
