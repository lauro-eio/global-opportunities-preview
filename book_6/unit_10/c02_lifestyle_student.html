Veridian Logistics: Community Connect Program

Read the following memo from Veridian Logistics. Don't worry about every detail, just try to understand the main purpose of the communication.

Materials

Veridian Logistics: Community Connect Program Launch

DATE: October 26, 2024
TO: All Employees
FROM: Maria Chen, Head of Corporate Social Responsibility
SUBJECT: Announcing Veridian Logistics’ New 'Community Connect' Program

Dear Team,

We are thrilled to launch 'Community Connect,' our new corporate social responsibility initiative designed to deepen our engagement with the local area. This program represents Veridian Logistics’ commitment to giving back and fostering stronger community ties. We believe that by investing in our shared environment, we empower both our community members and our employees.

Our initial focus areas, identified through stakeholder collaboration with local councils and residents, include neighborhood improvement projects and enhancements to local infrastructure. These might range from revitalizing public green spaces to supporting vocational training workshops for community members looking to gain new skills. We invite you to be a part of these meaningful volunteer initiatives.

We understand that participation in such programs requires careful consideration of personal schedules. Veridian Logistics is committed to fostering an environment where employees can maintain a healthy work-life balance. Therefore, all participation is entirely voluntary, and we encourage you to respect personal boundaries within your teams when discussing involvement. We ask that managers actively support flexible arrangements where feasible, ensuring that employees can prioritize personal commitments without undue pressure. If you are unable to participate, we appreciate your understanding; there will be future opportunities, and we anticipate many will choose to do so then.

More details on specific projects and sign-up procedures will follow next week. We look forward to building a stronger, more connected community together.

Sincerely,
Maria Chen

Tasks

What is the main purpose of this memo?

The primary aim of this memo is to:

  • Announce mandatory new work hours for community projects.
  • Introduce a new company program for community engagement and invite voluntary participation.
  • Request ideas for new community projects from employees.
  • Inform employees about recent changes in company policy regarding personal leave.

Your Role in Community & Your Work-Life Balance

Imagine you are a team leader discussing the 'Community Connect' program with your colleagues. Your goal is to encourage participation while also ensuring everyone feels comfortable and respected.

Consider two colleagues:
1. One colleague expresses hesitation due to existing personal commitments.
2. Another colleague is very enthusiastic but starts asking personal questions about other people's weekend plans and availability.

How would you navigate this conversation? Discuss with a partner how you would:
* Encourage participation without pressure.
* Address the enthusiastic colleague's over-eagerness politely.
* Reassure the hesitant colleague.

Try to use the phrases for professional demeanor and the structural techniques (like 'do so' or omitted clauses) you've learned to make your communication diplomatic and concise.
