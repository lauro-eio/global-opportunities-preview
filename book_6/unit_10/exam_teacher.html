1. q01lexis · class 1
The company's new initiative aims to improve staff satisfaction by offering substantial ______ to help employees afford living closer to the office.
  • A.housing subsidies ✓
  • B.travel stipends
  • C.relocation bonuses
  • D.rental assistance funds
ExplanationHousing subsidies are specific payments or financial aid designed to help individuals cover housing costs, aligning with the goal of living closer to the office.
