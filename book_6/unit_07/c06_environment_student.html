1. Have you noticed any green initiatives or changes towards sustainable practices in your current workplace or industry?

Understanding Workplace Green Initiatives

Read the internal report below about a company's transition to greener operations. Pay attention to the main points and how the information is presented.

Materials

Internal Update: Workforce Transition to Sustainable Practices

To: All Staff
From: Operations & Sustainability Department
Date: October 26, 2023
Subject: Workforce Transition to Sustainable Practices & New Opportunities

Dear Team,

As part of our company's commitment to environmental responsibility and economic diversification, we are actively transitioning towards more sustainable employment practices across all departments. This strategic shift is in response to global trends and growing demand within the renewable energy sector and related industries.

We understand that such a transition presents both opportunities and potential challenges. To support our valued employees, we are launching comprehensive upskilling programs designed to equip you with the necessary expertise for emerging green jobs within our organization and the wider economy.

Over the next six months, we will focus on:

1. Skill Gap Analysis: Identifying specific areas where current skills can be adapted or enhanced for new roles.
2. Training Modules: Developing practical, hands-on modules covering areas like energy efficiency, waste management, and sustainable logistics.
3. Career Pathways: Mapping out clear progression routes for individuals interested in specialized green jobs, such as sustainability coordinators, eco-logistics specialists, and renewable energy technicians.

We encourage all staff to actively participate in upcoming information sessions to learn more about these upskilling programs and how they can benefit your long-term career development. Your input is crucial as we navigate this exciting and essential transformation. Together, we can build a more resilient and environmentally conscious future for our company and community.

Sincerely,
The Operations & Sustainability Department

Drafting Your Green Economy Proposal

You work for a company that is beginning to explore green initiatives and sustainable employment practices. Write a short email or memo to your manager or team lead (around 150-200 words) proposing an idea or reporting on a relevant opportunity or challenge for your department.

Your writing should:

* Audience: Your manager or team lead.
* Purpose: To inform, propose, or suggest a course of action related to green transition/upskilling.
* Content: Focus on a specific green job opportunity, an upskilling program idea, or a challenge related to economic diversification in your sector.
* Length: Approximately 150-200 words.
* Tone: Professional and clear.

Try to include some of the key terms and phrases from the previous activity.

Reviewing and Improving Your Writing

Now, you will exchange your writing with a partner to give and receive feedback. Use the questions below to guide your review.

Part 1: Content Feedback (Focus on the message)

1. Clarity of Purpose: Is the main point (proposal, opportunity, challenge) of the email/memo clear? What is it?
2. Relevance: Does the content directly relate to the green economy and sustainable employment as intended?
3. Impact: Does the writer effectively communicate the potential benefits or challenges?
4. Audience: Is the tone appropriate for a manager or team lead? Is it professional?

Part 2: Language Feedback (Focus on accuracy and style)

1. Vocabulary: Are key green economy terms used effectively and accurately (e.g., upskilling programs, green jobs, renewable energy sector)?
2. Clarity & Flow: Are there any sentences or phrases that are unclear or could be expressed more effectively?
3. Professionalism: Does the language sound professional and appropriate for a workplace context?

After receiving feedback, revise your email or memo to improve it.
