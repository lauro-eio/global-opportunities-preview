Our Company, Our Planet

Imagine your company wants to cut operational costs and improve its environmental record. What practical green initiatives would you propose for your workplace?

2. Who would be involved in these initiatives?

Words for a Greener Workplace

Match the words and phrases on the left with their definitions on the right. These terms are important for understanding the text about workplace sustainability.

Vocabulary

  • energy efficiency
  • carbon footprint
  • corporate responsibility

Tasks

Match each term to its definition.

  • waste reduction — Minimizing the amount of trash or unwanted materials produced.
  • energy efficiency — Using less energy to perform the same task or provide the same service.
  • carbon footprint — The total amount of greenhouse gases (like CO2) caused by an organization, event, or product.
  • corporate responsibility — A business's duty to act ethically and contribute to economic development while improving the quality of life for its workforce, families, and society.
  • sustainable practices — Methods or actions that meet the needs of the present without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs.

Understanding the Proposal

Read the memo below quickly. Don't worry about understanding every word. Your goal is to get the main idea.

After reading, choose the best option to answer the question about the memo's primary purpose.

Materials

Update: Our Commitment to a Greener Workplace

Subject: Update: Our Commitment to a Greener Workplace
Date: October 26, 2023
From: The Green Initiatives Task Force

Dear Team,

As part of our ongoing commitment to corporate responsibility and improving our environmental record, the Green Initiatives Task Force is pleased to share an update on our progress and introduce some new proposals. Our goal is to implement practical, impactful changes that not only benefit the planet but also enhance our operational efficiency.

Over the past quarter, we have seen significant success in our waste reduction efforts. Thanks to everyone's participation in our new recycling program, we’ve cut down on landfill waste by 15%. This initiative proves that small, collective actions can make a big difference.

Looking ahead, we are focusing on two key areas: energy efficiency and reducing our overall carbon footprint. We are currently evaluating several proposals for upgraded LED lighting in all offices and warehouses, which promises substantial energy savings. Additionally, we are exploring options for carpooling programs and encouraging remote work where possible to minimize travel emissions. These steps are crucial for lowering our environmental impact.

We believe that by adopting more sustainable practices across all departments, we can set a new standard for our industry. This isn't just about compliance; it's about building a healthier, more resource-conscious future for our company and community.

We invite all employees to share their ideas and feedback on these initiatives. Your input is invaluable as we continue to shape a greener, more sustainable workplace. Please look out for upcoming workshops and discussion forums where you can learn more and contribute.

Thank you for your continued dedication.

Sincerely,
The Green Initiatives Task Force
