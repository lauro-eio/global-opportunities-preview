L-IN

Lead-In: Workplace Ethics Dilemmas

Teacher notes

1. Begin by asking learners about the 'lead-in brief' scenario directly.
2. Divide the class into small groups (2-3 learners) for discussion.
3. Ask them to consider the factors that influence their decision-making in such situations (e.g., impact on colleagues, personal values, company policy, job security).
4. Facilitate a brief whole-class feedback session to share perspectives, ensuring a professional and respectful tone.
5. Do not explicitly pre-teach any vocabulary or grammar at this stage.

Student handout

Navigating Professional Grey Areas

Think about a situation at work where you observed a minor practice that felt questionable or didn't quite align with what you believed was right. It wasn't a major offense, but it caused you to pause.

Discuss the following questions:

1. Would you address this observation? Why or why not?
2. What specific factors would influence your decision? (e.g., company culture, relationship with colleagues, potential impact, your personal moral compass)
3. How might addressing it, or not addressing it, affect your own professional identity within the team?

GIST

Gist Reading: Corporate Values & Professional Conduct

Teacher notes

1. Hand out the 'Professional Conduct Guidelines' memo to learners.
2. Instruct learners to read the memo quickly to get the main idea.
3. Explain that they do not need to understand every word at this stage.
4. Ask them to complete the multiple-choice question to check their overall understanding.
5. Monitor for completion and general comprehension, but do not provide answers yet.

Read the internal memo below from 'Corporate Standards'. Don't worry about understanding every detail right now. Just focus on getting the main message about the purpose of these guidelines. Then, answer the question below.

Materials

Memo: Upholding Our Shared Values & Professional Conduct

To: All Staff
From: Corporate Standards Department
Date: October 26, 2023
Subject: Reinforcing Our Commitment to Integrity and Professional Conduct

Dear Team,

In recent weeks, internal discussions have highlighted the critical importance of maintaining a consistent standard of professional conduct across all departments. While we are proud of our collaborative environment, it is imperative to periodically reinforce the foundational principles that define our workplace. This memo serves to reiterate key aspects of our Professional Conduct Policy, ensuring clarity and alignment with our corporate values.

What truly sets us apart is not just our collective achievements, but the ethical framework within which we operate. We recognize that navigating certain workplace situations can present a dilemma, and it is our collective accountability that ensures we uphold the highest standards. It is through vigilant adherence to our compliance codes that we establish a pluralistic and equitable environment for everyone.

We trust that each of you continues to embody the spirit of these guidelines. Your active participation in fostering a respectful and productive atmosphere is invaluable. Moving forward, my primary focus is to ensure that every team member feels empowered to contribute positively and address any observed practices that might subtly erode our shared integrity. We collectively strive for excellence, and I take pride in my contribution to this enduring culture.

Thank you for your ongoing commitment.

Sincerely,
The Corporate Standards Department

DET

Detailed Reading: Policy Specifics and Implied Meaning

Teacher notes

1. Ask learners to read the memo again, this time more carefully.
2. Instruct them to answer the short-text comprehension questions.
3. After they complete the tasks, put learners in pairs to compare their answers.
4. Facilitate a whole-class check for the explicit questions.
5. For the inference question, encourage discussion about what 'minor practices' might imply and how they relate to 'team cohesion' or 'stakeholder trust', connecting back to the L-IN.
6. Provide feedback on answers.

GD

Guided Discovery: Emphasizing and Articulating in Professional Contexts

Teacher notes

1. Direct learners to focus on specific sentences in the memo, first for cleft sentences.
2. Ask learners to identify the parts of the sentence that are being emphasized by 'It is...' or 'What... is...'. Elicit the rule that these structures highlight specific information.
3. Discuss the 'concept check questions' for cleft sentences: 'Are we emphasizing a specific ethical standard or cultural factor over others? Does this structure make the primary source of workplace friction or alignment stand out?'
4. Next, draw attention to the phrases for 'Articulating Professional Role and Team Contribution'. Ask learners to find examples in the text.
5. Discuss how these phrases convey commitment, value, and professional identity. Focus on the pragmatic effect: respectful, collaborative, clear contribution.
6. Highlight the stress patterns (phonological focus) for these phrases, modeling if necessary.

CP

Controlled Practice: Reinforcing Emphasis and Professional Identity

Teacher notes

1. Introduce the first set of tasks focusing on cleft sentences. Explain that learners need to rewrite sentences to emphasize the highlighted information using 'It is/was... that/who...' or 'What... is/was...'.
2. Provide an example if necessary. Guide learners to identify the element to be emphasized.
3. For the second set of tasks, instruct learners to complete the sentences using the professional role and team contribution phrases studied in the GD stage.
4. Encourage learners to work individually first, then compare answers in pairs.
5. Provide explicit correction and feedback on both grammatical accuracy of cleft sentences and the pragmatic appropriateness of the professional phrases.

FP

Free Practice: Addressing a Dilemma with Professional Diplomacy

Teacher notes

1. Remind learners of the initial scenario from the lead-in about observing a questionable practice at work.
2. Instruct them to imagine they need to write a short internal message (e.g., to a colleague, a small group, or as part of a suggestion for improvement) where they articulate their perspective on maintaining ethical standards or improving a specific process.
3. Emphasize the need to use cleft sentences to highlight key ethical principles and phrases for articulating professional role and team contribution to convey sincerity and collaboration.
4. Encourage them to focus on diplomatic and professional language.
5. Monitor during the writing process, providing support with language if needed.
6. For feedback, ask a few learners to share their messages with the class or in small groups. Provide constructive feedback, focusing on the appropriate use of target language and pragmatic effect.
Sample Answer:
'Subject: Upholding Our Collaborative Environment
Dear Team,
What truly strengthens our team is our shared commitment to transparency. It is a proactive approach to potential issues that ensures we maintain integrity. My primary focus is to ensure our projects run smoothly and ethically, and I take pride in my contribution to a fair process for all. Let's collectively strive for excellence in every interaction.'

Student handout

My Professional Stance: Communicating with Conviction

Imagine you are in a situation at work where you need to address a minor practice that you believe could negatively impact team morale or company standards, similar to the initial scenario we discussed. You want to communicate your perspective diplomatically and professionally.

Write a short paragraph (3-5 sentences) that could be part of an internal email, a team discussion point, or a suggestion for improvement. In your message:

* Use at least one cleft sentence (e.g., 'It is... that...' or 'What... is...') to emphasize a core ethical value or a critical aspect of professional conduct.
* Include at least one phrase that articulates your professional role or team contribution (e.g., 'I take pride in my contribution', 'My primary focus is to ensure...').
* Maintain a respectful and collaborative tone.
