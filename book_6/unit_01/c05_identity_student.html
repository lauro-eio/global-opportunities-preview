Bridging Communication Gaps at Work

Think about a time in your professional life when you had to explain a complex task, a new procedure, or an important company policy to someone from a very different background or perhaps with limited language proficiency.

1. What was the situation? Who was the person you were communicating with?
2. What specific adjustments did you make to your communication style or choice of words?
3. How did these adjustments help ensure the message was understood and respected?
4. Why is it important to adapt our communication in a diverse workplace?

Choosing Your Professional Voice

Read the internal memo below about a new company-wide 'Efficiency Drive'. Imagine you are a key team member or a department head drafting an email response to this initiative. For each point, choose the phrase that best reflects a professional and committed approach in your reply.

Materials

Internal Memo: Launching Our 'Efficiency Drive' Initiative

To: All Department Heads and Team Leads
From: Operations Management
Subject: Launching Our 'Efficiency Drive' Initiative
Date: 25th October

Dear Team,

We are excited to announce the launch of our new 'Efficiency Drive' initiative, aimed at optimizing our internal processes and enhancing overall productivity across all departments. This is a critical step to ensure our continued success and competitive edge in the market.

Over the next quarter, we will be implementing several strategic changes, including a review of current workflows, the introduction of new digital tools, and a renewed focus on cross-departmental collaboration. We understand that change can bring challenges, but we are confident that with your active participation and insights, this initiative will yield significant positive results.

We believe that every team member has a vital role to play in this transformation. Your feedback, commitment, and expertise are invaluable as we move forward. We will be holding a series of briefing sessions next week to provide more details and answer any questions you may have. Please review your departmental processes and come prepared to discuss how your team can contribute.

Thank you for your dedication and support.

Best regards,
Operations Management

We all try to do a good job for the company.

  • We all chip in to meet targets.

  • I stick with our rules.
  • I'm a big fan of our company's beliefs.

When stating your particular strength relevant to the initiative:

I'm good at the technical stuff.

  • I'm pretty handy with the technology.
  • My expertise lies in optimising technical workflows.
  • I know a lot about the machines.

Mastering Professional Language

In professional settings, the language we use significantly impacts how we are perceived. Choosing the right words helps convey confidence, commitment, and respect. Here are some key phrases for articulating your professional role and team contribution.

* I take pride in my contribution (meaning: I value my work and strive for quality)
* My primary focus is to ensure... (meaning: This is my main objective/priority)
* We collectively strive for excellence (meaning: As a team, we work hard to achieve high standards)
* I'm committed to upholding our values (meaning: I am dedicated to maintaining our principles)
* My expertise lies in [area] (meaning: This is where my specialized knowledge and skills are)

Consider the nuance and appropriateness of these expressions compared to more casual alternatives.

Presenting Your Project Proposal

You are part of a team that has identified a critical area for improvement in your workplace, and you've developed a new initiative or project to address it. Now, you need to present your proposal to a senior manager or a panel of stakeholders.

Working in pairs or small groups, prepare a short pitch (2-3 minutes) that:

1. Briefly describes your proposed initiative (e.g., a new safety protocol, an improved inventory system, a team training program).
2. Explains why it's important for the company.
3. Clearly articulates your team's role, commitment, and expertise in making this initiative a success.

Make sure to use the professional expressions we practiced in the lesson:

* I take pride in my contribution...
* My primary focus is to ensure...
* We collectively strive for excellence...
* I'm committed to upholding our values...
* My expertise lies in [area]...

Be ready to present your proposal to the class!
