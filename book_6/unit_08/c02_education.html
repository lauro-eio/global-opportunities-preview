Lead-In: Strategic Investment in the Workplace

Teacher notes

1. Elicit learners' experiences with workplace investment decisions regarding new equipment or software.
2. Encourage discussion around critical evaluation, not just acceptance of new tools.
3. Connect the conversation to the idea of assessing vendor claims and understanding the potential operational impact before making a decision.

Student handout

Thinking About New Tools

Think about the last time your workplace considered investing in a new piece of equipment or software. Discuss with a partner:

1. What were the most important questions you or your team had about it?
2. How did you determine if it would genuinely benefit your operations and fit your current needs?
3. Have you ever felt that vendor claims were overly optimistic or didn't quite match reality?

Student handout

First Look: New Software Claims

Read the internal memo below quickly. Don't worry about understanding every detail. Focus on identifying the main purpose of the memo.

Materials

Internal Memo: Initial Assessment of the 'OptiFlow' Logistics Software

To: Operations Management Team
From: Process Improvement Committee
Date: October 26, 2023
Subject: Initial Assessment of the 'OptiFlow' Logistics Software

Following the recent demonstration and preliminary review of the 'OptiFlow' Logistics Software, this memo outlines our initial findings and concerns. While the vendor's presentation emphasized significant operational impact and promised an immediate uplift in efficiency, our committee's equipment evaluation suggests a more cautious approach is warranted.

The vendor claims that 'OptiFlow' will streamline our inventory management by 30% and reduce dispatch errors by 25%. They also highlighted an integrated module designed to help staff upskill in a specific area of data analysis, asserting that it would significantly enhance one's skill set across the team. However, our initial cost-benefit analysis indicates that the projected savings may be offset by substantial implementation costs and potential disruption during the transition period.

Specifically, we question the ease of integration with our legacy systems. The vendor suggests that our existing databases be migrated over a two-week period. Our IT department, however, demands that this be a phased approach to minimize system downtime. Furthermore, the mandatory training program for 'OptiFlow' users appears extensive. Staff will need to undertake professional development that goes beyond a simple orientation; indeed, some may need to receive mandatory training for a new certification in 'OptiFlow' operation. This represents a considerable commitment of time and resources.

We acknowledge the potential for process improvement, but we believe it is crucial that we do not rush into a decision based solely on optimistic projections. The committee recommends that a pilot program be initiated in a smaller department before a full-scale rollout. It is also imperative that we demand that the vendor provide a detailed integration plan that addresses our specific infrastructure. The overall objective is to ensure that any new investment genuinely supports our long-term goals and contributes positively to our team's capabilities, rather than creating unforeseen bottlenecks or requiring substantial, unbudgeted professional development initiatives.

We will reconvene next week to discuss these points further.

Tasks

What is the main purpose of this memo?

  • To endorse the immediate adoption of the OptiFlow software.
  • To express concerns and recommend further evaluation of the OptiFlow software.
  • To schedule a mandatory training session for the OptiFlow software.
  • To request a new budget for operational improvements.

GD

Guided Discovery: Formal Recommendations and Professional Growth

Teacher notes

1. Part 1: Mandative Subjunctive
- Direct learners to the highlighted verbs in the provided sentences from the memo.
- Elicit their observations about the form (bare infinitive despite subject) and the function (strong recommendations/requirements).
- Explain that this is the mandative subjunctive, used for formal, high-stakes suggestions or demands.
- Discuss the examples and guide them to articulate the meaning of each statement.
2. Part 2: Professional Learning & Development Terminology
- Instruct learners to match the professional development terms to their definitions.
- Review answers and discuss when and why these terms would be used in a workplace context (pragmatics).

Match the professional development terms from the memo with their definitions or examples.

  • upskill in a specific area — To learn new skills or improve existing ones in a particular field, often with a clear focus.
  • undertake professional development — To engage in activities designed to improve one's professional knowledge or skills, showing initiative.
  • receive mandatory training — To be required by the organization to complete specific instruction or courses, often for compliance or safety.
  • gain a new certification — To officially obtain a document or qualification proving competence in a specific skill or field.
  • enhance one's skill set — To improve, broaden, or add to the range of abilities and competencies one possesses.
CP

Controlled Practice: Crafting Formal Statements

Teacher notes

1. Task 1: Mandative Subjunctive
- Explain that learners need to rewrite sentences using the mandative subjunctive structure (Subject + academic verb + that + Subject + bare infinitive).
- Emphasize the removal of 'must', 'should', or changing verb conjugations to the bare infinitive.
2. Task 2: Professional Learning Terminology
- Instruct learners to choose the most appropriate professional development term to complete each sentence.
- Review answers and discuss nuances between similar terms.

FP

Freer Practice: Proposing Workplace Changes

Teacher notes

1. Instruct learners to imagine a new initiative or investment at their workplace.
2. Encourage them to discuss with a partner, using the mandative subjunctive and professional development vocabulary to formulate their recommendations and demands.
3. Monitor for correct usage of the target language.
4. Facilitate a short class feedback session where pairs can share one or two of their recommendations.

Sample Answers:
- "I suggest that the new system be piloted in a single department first."
- "It is crucial that all affected staff receive mandatory training."
- "Our team demands that the new machinery be thoroughly inspected by an independent body."
- "We recommend that employees undertake professional development to upskill in a specific area related to data analytics."

Student handout

Making Your Case: New Initiatives

Imagine your workplace is considering a significant new initiative or investment – perhaps new machinery, software, or a major change in operational procedures. Think about what your team or department would need to ensure its success.

In pairs, discuss and formulate formal recommendations and demands. Use the mandative subjunctive (e.g., 'I suggest that...') and vocabulary for professional development (e.g., 'receive mandatory training', 'upskill in a specific area') in your discussion.

Consider:
1. What formal recommendations or demands would you make to ensure the success and smooth implementation of this investment?
2. What kind of training or professional development would be essential for your team, and how would you formally articulate these needs?
