Lead-In: Addressing Persistent Workplace Challenges

Teacher notes

Introduce the concept of recurring workplace issues and the need for deeper analysis.
Encourage learners to share personal experiences from their professional contexts.
Facilitate a short group discussion around the provided questions.
Emphasize moving beyond superficial solutions to root causes, setting the stage for professional language related to development.

Student handout

Getting to the Root of the Problem

Think about a problem at your workplace that keeps coming back, even after people try to fix it. It's like a leaky pipe that someone just puts a bucket under, instead of fixing the leak itself.

1. What's an example of such a recurring problem you've encountered?
2. Why do you think these problems are hard to solve permanently?
3. How would you start a discussion with your team to stop patching things up and really find out why the problem is happening? What kind of skills would you need to lead that discussion effectively?

Read the internal memo from the Head of Operations regarding professional development opportunities. Then, complete the matching activity to show your current understanding of how to express development goals professionally.

Materials

Memo: Enhancing Our Team's Capabilities

Subject: Enhancing Our Team's Capabilities: Professional Development Initiative

Dear Team,

We are excited to launch a new initiative aimed at fostering continuous growth and improving our collective expertise. As part of our commitment to your career progression and the company's success, we are providing various opportunities for professional development over the next quarter.

This program encourages all team members to consider how they might upskill in a specific area that aligns with their current role or future aspirations within the company. For certain roles, there may be a requirement to receive mandatory training to ensure compliance with new safety regulations or updated software protocols.

We also support individuals who wish to gain a new certification that directly benefits their job function or enhances their strategic value to the team. Ultimately, the goal is for each of you to enhance one's skill set, contributing to both your personal growth and our operational efficiency.

Please review the attached catalogue of courses and workshops. We encourage you to discuss your development plans with your immediate supervisor.

Best regards,
[Manager's Name]
Head of Operations

TCH

Teach: Professional Learning & Development Terminology

Teacher notes

Go through the target exponents from T1.
For each exponent:
- Elicit or explain its meaning using examples relevant to the learners' workplace context.
- Clarify its register (professional, formal workplace language) and typical collocations.
- Highlight the word stress for each collocation (e.g., UPskill, proFESsional deVELOPment, MANdatory TRAINing). Drill pronunciation.
- Use CCQs to check understanding (e.g., 'If it's 'mandatory training', do you have a choice?', 'Does 'upskill' mean you already have some skills or none at all?').
Provide the column banks for reference and then set the multiple-choice task for reinforced learning.

T2

Test 2: Professional Development Dialogue - Production

Teacher notes

Divide learners into pairs.
Assign roles: Employee and Supervisor.
Brief the Employee to prepare what they will say to discuss their development, focusing on integrating the target language naturally.
Brief the Supervisor to ask probing questions about the employee's development plans and how it aligns with company goals.
Monitor carefully for accurate use of the target exponents, correct register, and appropriate word stress.
Provide constructive feedback, noting effective usage and areas for improvement.
Encourage self-correction and peer-correction during the activity.
Model Response (for teacher's reference):
Employee: 'Thank you for meeting with me. I've been reflecting on my career trajectory here, and I'm keen to upskill in a specific area related to project management software. I'd like to undertake professional development by enrolling in the advanced PMP preparation course. My ultimate goal is to gain a new certification in project management, as I believe this will significantly enhance my skill set and allow me to contribute more effectively to upcoming projects, especially in leading our team through systematic inquiries to uncover root causes.'

Student handout

Planning Your Path Forward

Imagine you are meeting with your supervisor to discuss your career growth. You want to request specific training or development opportunities. Using the professional language we've just practiced, prepare what you would say to:

1. Explain a specific area where you want to improve your skills.
2. Propose a type of professional development you'd like to undertake.
3. Mention if there's a particular certification you aim to gain.
4. Explain how this will enhance your overall skill set and benefit the team, particularly in addressing persistent workplace problems (as we discussed earlier).

Work with a partner. One of you plays the employee, the other the supervisor. Then swap roles. Focus on using the target phrases naturally and professionally, paying attention to word stress for clarity.
