Lead-In: Digital Influence at Work

Teacher notes

Introduce the lesson by connecting to learners' real-world experiences. Guide discussion on how subtle language can influence decisions in professional contexts. Encourage examples from their workplaces or personal experiences with marketing or internal communications.

Student handout

Spotting Hidden Messages

Think about recent communications you've seen, either at work (memos, emails) or outside of work (advertisements, public service announcements).
1. Did you notice any parts that seemed designed to persuade you or influence your feelings or actions in a subtle way?
2. What specific words, phrases, or images made you feel that way?
3. How might recognizing these techniques be useful in your professional life?

MOD

Model Analysis: Unpacking a Persuasive Memo

Teacher notes

Ask students to read the memo. The focus is not on perfect comprehension but on identifying the persuasive or manipulative techniques used. Guide them to pinpoint specific phrases and discuss the intended emotional or psychological effect on the reader. Prompt them to consider the real implications behind the 'positive' language.

PREP

Preparation: Crafting Responses to Manipulative Language

Teacher notes

Guide students through the column banks. Discuss the purpose of each type of phrase.
1. Focus on how to professionally identify manipulative language without being accusatory.
2. Emphasize the importance of using neutral and factual language.
3. Practice using the professional objection phrases to soften the impact of concerns.

FP

Freer Practice: Responding to a Workplace Initiative

Teacher notes

Provide a sample answer for the writing task, demonstrating a professional, non-confrontational response to the manipulative memo.
Sample Answer:
Subject: Query Regarding 'Team Optimization Initiative'
Dear [Manager's Name],
I am writing to you regarding the recently announced 'Team Optimization Initiative' and the introduction of a more flexible scheduling system. While I appreciate the intention to enhance operational efficiency, I have some concerns regarding the potential impact on staff routines and personal commitments. Could we explore in more detail how the 'optimal resource allocation' will specifically affect individual shifts and stability? It would be beneficial to consider the practical implications on workforce planning to ensure a smooth transition. To ensure successful implementation and continued team morale, clearer guidelines on the new scheduling system would be helpful. Thank you for your time and consideration.
Best regards,
[Your Name]

Student handout

Drafting Your Professional Response

Write a short email or memo (100-150 words) to your department manager or a relevant stakeholder in response to the 'Team Optimization Initiative' memo you analyzed.

Your Goal: To express your initial thoughts or concerns professionally, using neutral and factual language, avoiding any manipulative tactics yourself. Focus on asking for clarification, proposing practical considerations, or highlighting potential challenges, rather than direct criticism.

Audience: Your department manager/relevant stakeholders.
Purpose: To engage critically and professionally with the proposed changes.
Length: Approximately 100-150 words.

FBDC

Feedback and Correction: Refining Your Professional Voice

Teacher notes

Guide learners through peer feedback (if appropriate) or self-assessment.
1. Focus feedback first on content: Did they address the task? Is the tone appropriate? Are the points clear and professional?
2. Then move to language: Review vocabulary choice, sentence structure, and overall clarity.
3. Highlight effective use of phrases from the PREP stage.
4. Provide constructive criticism on any remaining manipulative or overly emotional language in their responses.

Student handout

Reviewing Your Professional Communication

Exchange your response with a partner or review your own writing. Consider the following questions:
1. Did you clearly express your concerns or questions without being overly emotional or confrontational?
2. Did you use neutral and factual language? Identify any phrases that could be rephrased for more professionalism.
3. Is your tone appropriate for communicating with management about a new initiative?
4. What is one thing you did well, and one area where your writing could be improved?
