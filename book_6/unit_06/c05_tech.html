Lead-In: Industry News & Digital Responsibility

Teacher notes

1. Introduce the topic by eliciting student experiences with industry news or reports relevant to their sector.
2. Encourage students to share their initial strategies for evaluating the credibility and potential biases of online information.
3. Guide the discussion to highlight the importance of critical thinking and professional judgment when assessing information that could impact their work or company.
4. Do not pre-teach vocabulary.

Finally, a core principle of our work involves keeping sensitive client information safe (5). Always follow protocols to prevent any breaches.

Your cooperation in upholding these standards is greatly appreciated.

Sincerely,
The HR Department

Teach: Professional Digital Discourse

Teacher notes

1. Go through the answers to Test 1, eliciting the correct professional phrases.
2. For each exponent, clarify its meaning, emphasizing the nuances that make it more appropriate for a professional context compared to the informal alternative. Elicit CCQs such as: 'Can you use "digital footprint" when talking about a child's online activity, or is it more for a professional context?' (Professional) 'Would you say "deal with too much info" in a formal report?' (No) 'What does 'leverage' imply that 'use' doesn't?' (Strategic, effective use of resources).
3. Focus on phonology: Model and drill clear word stress within these collocations and a generally falling intonation to convey assertiveness and professionalism in workplace discussions (e.g., manage your digital footprint, maintain a professional online presence).
4. Guide students through the matching task to consolidate understanding of meaning and context.

Student handout

Building Your Digital Vocabulary

Now let's look at the language used in professional digital communication. The phrases below are common in workplace discussions about technology and online conduct. They help to convey clarity, neutrality, and professionalism.

Match the professional phrases with their detailed meanings and the specific contexts in which they are most appropriately used.

Materials

Tasks

Match the professional phrases with their definitions and appropriate contexts. Focus on why these phrases are preferred in formal or workplace settings.

  • manage your digital footprint — To control the information about you that exists on the internet, typically referring to professional reputation.
  • maintain a professional online presence — To actively shape how you are perceived online, especially in contexts related to your career or company.
  • address information overload — To systematically deal with an excessive amount of data or communications, often through strategic methods.
  • leverage digital tools effectively — To utilize digital software, platforms, or systems in a way that maximizes their benefits for specific goals.
  • ensure data privacy — To guarantee the protection of personal or sensitive data from unauthorized access or misuse.
T2

Test 2: Discussing Digital Best Practices

Teacher notes

1. Divide students into small groups (2-3) or pairs.
2. Explain the scenario: a team meeting to discuss digital strategies. Emphasize that they should use the professional language learned in the lesson.
3. Instruct them to discuss the provided points, integrating the target exponents naturally into their conversation.
4. Monitor groups closely, listening for accurate usage, appropriate register, and correct pronunciation of the target exponents.
5. Provide delayed, constructive feedback on language use, focusing on the C1-level lexis and its pragmatic appropriacy.
6. Model answer/key points for discussion (example sentences):
- 'To manage our digital footprint effectively, we should regularly review our social media settings and company website contributions.'
- 'It's crucial to maintain a professional online presence by adhering to company guidelines for all external communications.'
- 'One strategy to address information overload could be to implement a clearer email sorting system and designated reading times.'
- 'We could leverage digital tools effectively by integrating our project management software with communication platforms for seamless updates.'
- 'To ensure data privacy, we must strictly follow the updated protocols for handling sensitive client information, especially when working remotely.'

Strategizing for Digital Success

Imagine your team is holding a brief meeting to discuss strategies for improving your department's use of digital technologies and handling online information professionally. Your manager has asked everyone to contribute ideas, using the professional language we've discussed.

Discuss the following:
1. How can we collectively manage our digital footprint and maintain a professional online presence as a team?
2. What steps can we take to address information overload from emails, reports, and industry news?

4. How do we reinforce procedures to ensure data privacy for our clients and internal operations?
