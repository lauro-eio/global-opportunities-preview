Lead-In: Workplace Digital Activity

Teacher notes

1. Greet learners and explain the theme of the lesson: workplace digital monitoring and data privacy.
2. Introduce the concept of companies monitoring digital activity.
3. Ask learners to discuss the lead-in brief questions in pairs or small groups.
4. Encourage them to share any personal experiences or observations about policies in their current or past workplaces.
5. Facilitate a brief whole-class discussion, noting common themes or differing opinions.

Gist Reading: Navigating Digital Conduct Policy

First Read: Understanding the Policy's Core Message

Read the policy brief below. Don't worry about every detail or unknown words yet. Focus on understanding the main purpose of the document. Then, answer the question.

Materials

NexusCorp Digital Conduct Policy Brief

DATE: [Current Date]
TO: All NexusCorp Employees
FROM: HR Department
SUBJECT: Clarifying Our Digital Conduct & Monitoring Policy

At NexusCorp, we are committed to fostering a productive and secure work environment. This brief serves to reiterate and clarify our existing policies regarding digital conduct, workplace monitoring, and data privacy. It is crucial that all employees understand their responsibilities and rights in this digitally-connected workplace.

Our policies are designed to ensure data privacy for company assets and client information, protect against security threats, and maintain a professional work atmosphere. To achieve these objectives, NexusCorp employs strategically-implemented surveillance systems that monitor company-issued devices and network activity. This includes, but is not limited to, email communications, internet browsing history, and usage of internal systems. Such algorithmically-driven insights help us identify and address potential security vulnerabilities and policy breaches.

Employees are expected to maintain a professional online presence both within and outside working hours, especially when their activities may reflect on the company's reputation. This also involves responsibly managing your digital footprint on company platforms. Any personally-identifiable information collected through monitoring is handled strictly in accordance with local and international data privacy regulations and our internal HR policy.

We understand that questions may arise regarding these measures. NexusCorp is dedicated to upholding employee rights while safeguarding company interests. We encourage an open dialogue and invite employees to review the full Digital Conduct Policy document available on the intranet. Our aim is to leverage digital tools effectively while fostering trust and transparency. Your cooperation in adhering to these guidelines is greatly appreciated as we collectively address information overload and ensure a secure, respectful digital workplace.

GD

Guided Discovery: Precision in Professional Discourse

Teacher notes

1. Draw learners' attention to specific sentences or phrases from the policy brief that exemplify the target linguistic structures and pragmatic uses.
2. For Complex Modifier Compounding: Highlight examples like 'strategically-implemented surveillance systems' and 'algorithmically-driven insights'. Ask learners to identify the parts (adverb, participle, noun) and discuss the effect of the hyphen and the precision it adds. Contrast with non-hyphenated versions to show loss of clarity.
3. For Professional Digital Discourse: Point out phrases like 'maintain a professional online presence', 'manage your digital footprint', 'ensure data privacy'. Ask learners to explain what these mean in context and why they are effective in a professional document.
4. Use the match_pairs task to connect collocations with their professional meaning.
5. Facilitate a discussion on the importance of such precise and formal language in high-stakes documents like HR policies, focusing on workplace dignity and clarity.

FP

Freer Practice: Discussing Policy Modifications

Teacher notes

1. Introduce the freer practice task, explaining that learners will apply the language from the lesson to a more open-ended, high-stakes scenario.
2. Divide learners into small groups or pairs.
3. Instruct them to discuss the prompt, encouraging them to use the complex modifier compounds and professional digital discourse phrases as naturally as possible.
4. Circulate, monitor discussions, and note down good examples of target language use, as well as any persistent errors.
5. Facilitate a whole-class feedback session, inviting groups to share their modifications and justifications.
6. Provide constructive feedback on language use, focusing on precision, formality, and appropriate register.
7. Sample Answer Points: Highlight specific areas of the policy (e.g., 'more clearly-defined monitoring protocols', 'proactively-managed digital identities'). Encourage use of phrases like 'upholding employee rights', 'fostering transparency', 'addressing concerns about privacy'.
