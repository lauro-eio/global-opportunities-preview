TCH

Teach: Clarifying Grammatical Nominalization

Teacher notes

1. Begin by reviewing the errors identified in T1, specifically highlighting how students used verbs where nominalized forms would be more appropriate for C2 formal contexts.
2. Introduce or re-clarify the meaning of grammatical nominalization: 'Converting action-based verbs into complex abstract noun packages to construct authoritative, impersonal scientific assessments concerning anthropocene tracking and environmental bio-governance.'
3. Explain the form: 'Abstract noun phrase + active verb + prepositional complement' (e.g., The acceleration of global resource degradation necessitates bio-governance). Provide both affirmative and negative examples as per the linguistic target.
4. Use the concept_check_questions to guide student understanding: 'Have we packaged an action into a complex noun phrase to sound more formal and objective?' and 'Is the sentence focused on a specific human worker, or on an objective systemic process?'
5. Use the column banks to visually demonstrate common verb-to-noun transformations.
6. Guide students through the practice tasks, encouraging them to apply the clarified form and meaning.

Student handout

Packaging Actions into Powerful Nouns

In C2-level professional communication, especially for policy, scientific, or executive reports, we often use grammatical nominalization to make our language sound more formal, objective, and authoritative. Instead of using verbs to describe actions, we 'package' these actions into abstract noun phrases. This shifts the focus from who performs an action to the action or process itself.

Key idea: Convert verbs into noun phrases to achieve an impersonal and objective tone, often for describing systemic processes or findings.

T2

Test 2: Drafting Workplace Policy Statements

Teacher notes

1. Divide students into small groups (3-4).
2. Reiterate the situational_focus: crafting and implementing effective workplace sustainability policies.
3. Instruct groups to select two areas from the thematic_context (e.g., digital carbon footprint, paperless initiatives, eco-procurement) and draft one formal policy statement for each, using grammatical nominalization.
4. Emphasize that each statement should be a single, well-structured sentence, reflecting the C2 level and the impersonal, authoritative tone discussed.
5. Circulate and monitor for correct application of nominalization, C2 lexis (e.g., digital carbon footprint reduction, resource degradation mitigation, sustainable procurement protocols), and overall coherence.
6. Encourage peer review within groups before sharing.
7. Sample expected output (for teacher reference): 'The implementation of digital carbon footprint reduction initiatives has led to a significant decrease in energy consumption across all departments.' or 'The non-adherence to eco-procurement office standards will necessitate further review of supplier contracts.'
