Understanding the New Environmental Mandate

Read the following internal directive from the CEO regarding our new environmental commitments. Focus on understanding the overall purpose and main message.

Materials

Internal Directive: Advancing Our Environmental Stewardship

To: All Department Heads
From: CEO's Office
Date: [Current Date]
Subject: Enhanced Commitment to Green Facility Management and Resource Efficiency Initiatives

Dear Colleagues,

Our commitment to environmental responsibility is a cornerstone of our corporate values and operational strategy. As part of our ongoing efforts to uphold global stewardship, I am announcing a significant enhancement to our approach to environmental impact assessment and carbon footprint mitigation across all facilities.

Effective immediately, we will be implementing a comprehensive program focused on sustainable operational practices. This initiative will involve a thorough review of our current resource consumption patterns, including detailed energy audits across all commercial properties. The objective is to identify key areas for improvement and integrate smart building tech solutions to optimize our energy usage.

Furthermore, we will be initiating a company-wide drive towards waste stream optimization, with a particular focus on improving waste segregation protocols. This is not merely about compliance with environmental regulations; it is about embracing a proactive stance in minimizing our ecological footprint.

We anticipate that the acceleration of global resource degradation necessitates a robust response from all sectors. Our collective effort in adhering to environmental regulations and going beyond minimum requirements will position us as a leader in responsible green facility management. Department heads are required to review this directive and prepare for upcoming training sessions on new protocols and reporting standards. Your proactive engagement is crucial for the successful implementation of energy-efficient upgrades and the achievement of our ambitious sustainability goals.

Sincerely,
[CEO's Name]

GD

Guided Discovery: Nominalization and Formal Lexis

Teacher notes

1. For Task A (Nominalization): The answer to both concept check questions is 'Yes'. The focus shifts from a specific human agent to an objective, systemic process or concept. This creates a more formal, authoritative, and impersonal tone suitable for policy documents or scientific assessments, aligning with the target meaning of nominalization. It objectifies the issue, making it seem less about individual choice and more about unavoidable systemic facts.
2. For Task B (Professional Lexis): Check learners' answers for the specific exponents. Emphasize correct pronunciation with primary stress often on the first significant word (e.g., sustainable, carbon, adhering, energy). Clarify that these phrases are crucial for professional communication, particularly in high-stakes contexts like environmental reporting.

Student handout

Deconstructing Formal Language in Sustainability Reports

Now, let's look at how this directive uses language to convey its message formally and authoritatively. We'll examine two key features:

A. Grammatical Nominalization: Turning Actions into Concepts
Look at the phrases below from the text. Notice how verbs or adjectives are transformed into noun phrases. This makes the language sound more formal and focuses on the process or concept rather than who is doing the action.

1. 'Our collective effort in adhering to environmental regulations and going beyond minimum requirements...' (Original action: 'We adhere to environmental regulations')
2. '...the successful implementation of energy-efficient upgrades...' (Original action: 'We implement energy-efficient upgrades')
3. '...the acceleration of global resource degradation necessitates a robust response...' (Original action: 'Global resources are degrading faster')

Task A: For each of the nominalized phrases above, consider: Have we packaged an action into a complex noun phrase to sound more formal and objective? Is the sentence focused on a specific human worker, or on an objective systemic process? How does this change the tone and impact of the message?

Free Practice: Proposing Environmental Initiatives

Teacher notes

Sample Answer:

Executive Summary: Strategic Environmental Enhancements

This proposal outlines critical initiatives for the continuous improvement of our corporate environmental stewardship. The acceleration of global resource degradation necessitates proactive measures, ensuring our operations align with leading sustainable operational practices. Firstly, a comprehensive energy audit across all facilities is proposed, targeting the identification of energy drains and subsequent implementation of energy-efficient upgrades. This will lead to significant carbon footprint mitigation. Secondly, the optimization of waste streams through enhanced waste segregation protocols is vital. This will reduce disposal costs and improve our overall ecological impact assessment. Such actions are not merely about adhering to environmental regulations but signify our commitment to responsible corporate governance and long-term resource efficiency initiatives.
