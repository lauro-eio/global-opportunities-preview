5. q05lexis · class 2

  • B.thereof ✓
  • C.wherein
  • D.hereby
Explanation'Thereof' means 'of that' or 'of it,' referring to the agreement. 'Herein' means 'in this document,' 'wherein' means 'in which,' and 'hereby' means 'by this means,' which are not appropriate in this context for specifying the date of the agreement itself.

28. q28grammar · class 7
The amendments to the contract must be executed ______ the specified deadline.
  • A.by
  • B.within ✓
  • C.on
  • D.before
Explanation'Within' implies that the action must be completed during the period leading up to the deadline, or before the end of that period, which is a precise legal and formal way to express a timeframe for completion, especially used with 'deadline'. 'By' implies 'no later than', but 'within' emphasizes the enclosed nature of the timeframe. 'On' is too specific to a date, and 'before' is also plausible but less precise than 'within' when dealing with a 'deadline' as a boundary.
