Teacher notes

1. Set the context by asking students to consider a personal scenario related to needing legal advice but facing financial barriers.
2. Elicit initial ideas about 'first port of call' and write them on the board (e.g., family, friends, union, online search).

Teach: Formal Language for Official Contexts

Teacher notes

1. Begin by checking answers to T1. For each correct answer, explicitly state the target exponent and its meaning.
2. Meaning and Register: Use the column_banks to highlight the meaning and contrast with less formal alternatives. Elicit or provide definitions and contexts for each target phrase.
* adhere strictly to protocols: 'To follow rules or procedures very carefully and without deviation.' (Vs. 'stick to the rules')
* lodge a formal complaint: 'To officially submit a written complaint.' (Vs. 'make a complaint')
* document the incident thoroughly: 'To record all details of an event in a comprehensive and systematic way.' (Vs. 'write down everything that happened')
* seek legal counsel: 'To ask a lawyer for professional advice.' (Vs. 'get advice from a lawyer')
* in full compliance with statutory requirements: 'Completely following all laws and regulations.' (Vs. 'doing everything by the law')
3. Pragmatics: Discuss why these formal expressions are preferred in the given context (e.g., credibility, clarity, legal protection, professionalism). Ask CCQs: 'Can you use 'stick to the rules' in a formal memo? Why not?' 'What impression does 'lodge a formal complaint' give compared to 'report a problem'?'
4. Phonology: Focus on the pronunciation of multi-syllabic words within the phrases. Model and drill:
* adhere /ədˈhɪər/, strictly /ˈstrɪktli/, protocols /ˈproʊtəkɒlz/
* lodge /lɒdʒ/, formal /ˈfɔːməl/, complaint /kəmˈpleɪnt/
* document /ˈdɒkjʊmənt/, incident /ˈɪnsɪdənt/, thoroughly /ˈθʌrəli/
* seek /siːk/, legal /ˈliːɡəl/, counsel /ˈkaʊnsəl/
* compliance /kəmˈplaɪəns/, statutory /ˈstætʃətəri/, requirements /rɪˈkwaɪərmənts/
* Emphasize falling intonation on declarative statements (e.g., 'You must adhere strictly to protocols.') to convey authority and finality. Drill students repeating sentences with correct stress and intonation.
5. Write phrases on the board and encourage students to practice saying them.

T2

Test 2: Reporting a Workplace Issue

Teacher notes

1. Set the scene for the freer practice activity. Explain the scenario: students must draft a formal email/report about a safety concern in a logistics company.
2. Emphasize the need to use at least three of the target formal expressions and maintain a professional tone.
3. Give students a clear time limit (e.g., 10-12 minutes) to draft their emails individually.
4. Monitor as students write, offering support with vocabulary and syntax but avoiding direct error correction on target items unless a student is completely stuck. Note down common errors or excellent uses of the target language.
5. After the drafting phase, have students exchange their emails with a partner to check for clarity, tone, and appropriate use of the formal expressions.
6. Elicit a few examples from the class (or read out a few anonymous examples you've noted) and provide feedback on the use of target language and overall register. Reinforce phonology points if necessary.

Model Answer Example (for teacher reference):

Subject: Urgent – Safety Protocol Concerns regarding Loading Equipment

To: [Line Manager Name], [HR Department]
From: [Your Name]
Date: [Current Date]

Dear [Line Manager Name] and HR Department,

I am writing to formally express significant concerns regarding the recurrent issues with safety checks on loading equipment within the warehouse. Despite previous informal discussions, I regret to inform you that these protocols are not being adhered strictly to. This oversight poses a substantial risk of serious workplace accidents and potential injury to staff.

I feel it is my responsibility to document the incident thoroughly and bring this critical matter to your immediate attention. It is imperative that we operate in full compliance with statutory requirements to ensure a safe environment for all employees. I urge you to investigate this matter promptly and implement corrective measures.

Yours sincerely,
[Your Name]
