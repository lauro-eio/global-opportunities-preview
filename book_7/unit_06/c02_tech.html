Understanding the Ethical Framework

Read the following corporate statement from 'Synapse Solutions' regarding their approach to AI in customer interaction. Focus on understanding the primary message and the company's overarching commitment. Don't worry about every detail for now.

Materials

Synapse Solutions: Upholding Integrity in AI Customer Interfaces

At Synapse Solutions, we understand that deploying Artificial Intelligence in customer-facing roles involves navigating a complex ethical landscape. Our commitment to fostering responsible innovation is paramount, ensuring that technological advancements enhance, rather than diminish, human interaction and trust.

Recently, concerns have been raised about the impact of AI on customer experience, particularly regarding transparency and fairness. To address these ethical implications, we have established a robust governance framework. This framework is designed to actively promote upholding data privacy and rigorous protocols for addressing algorithmic bias across all our AI systems.

We recognize the potential for an empathy gap when human interaction is minimized. Consequently, our AI interfaces are not designed to fully replace human agents but to augment their capabilities, handling automated triage for common inquiries and streamlining support processes. Under no circumstances do our internal guidelines permit AI systems to make critical, life-altering decisions without direct human oversight and intervention.

Furthermore, out of a commitment to customer sovereignty arose our new data ethics board. This board is tasked with ensuring full data sovereignty for our users and maintaining transparency in how their information is processed. We believe that clarity regarding AI's capabilities and limitations is crucial for building lasting trust.

Our escalation protocols are meticulously designed to ensure that any complex or sensitive customer query is promptly routed to a human specialist. We are continually refining our AI models through extensive feedback loops and ethical audits to ensure they align with our core values of respect, integrity, and service excellence. Our vision for AI is one that prioritizes human dignity and augments our ability to serve effectively, not to distance us from those we serve.

Tasks

What is the primary message of Synapse Solutions' corporate statement?

The main point of the statement is to communicate that Synapse Solutions:

  • Is planning to replace all human customer service agents with AI technology.
  • Is solely focused on using AI to reduce operational costs.
  • Is committed to ethical AI deployment that prioritizes customer trust and human oversight.
  • Has faced significant legal challenges due to its AI systems.

GD

Guided Discovery: Analyzing Discourse Patterns for Emphasis and Authority

Teacher notes

1. Direct learners to focus on specific sentences from the text, highlighting phrases that stand out.
2. Guide them to notice the unusual word order in 'Under no circumstances do our internal guidelines permit...' and 'Out of a commitment to customer sovereignty arose our new data ethics board.'
3. Ask concept check questions: 'Is the word order altered to put the focal pre-modifier context first? (Yes)', 'Does a negative fronted phrase like 'under no circumstances' require subject-auxiliary inversion? (Yes)'.
4. Explain that this structure (Prepositional Noun Phrase Fronting for Algorithmic Governance Analysis) is used to give emphasis and convey a high level of formality and authority, typical in corporate or executive reports.
5. Also, draw attention to the multi-word lexical items used to discuss ethical AI (e.g., ethical implications, addressing algorithmic bias). Discuss their precise meaning and how they contribute to a professional and authoritative tone.

Student handout

Spotting Strategic Language in Corporate Statements

Corporate statements often use specific language and sentence structures to convey importance, formality, and commitment. Look at these sentences from the Synapse Solutions statement:

1. "Under no circumstances do our internal guidelines permit AI systems to make critical, life-altering decisions without direct human oversight and intervention."
2. "Out of a commitment to customer sovereignty arose our new data ethics board."

Consider the word order in these sentences.

a) How do these sentences start differently from typical English sentences?
b) What effect does this unusual word order have? Does it make the statement sound more or less formal? More or less emphatic?
c) What is the general rule for word order when a negative phrase like 'under no circumstances' starts a sentence?

Now, identify other phrases in the text that express strong ethical principles or actions regarding AI. For example: ethical implications, addressing algorithmic bias, upholding data privacy, fostering responsible innovation.

2. Provide sentence prompts that require transforming basic statements into more formal, emphatic corporate-style language.
3. Model the first example if necessary.

5. Provide explicit correction and feedback, referring back to the target forms.

FP

Freer Practice: Drafting an Executive Briefing on AI Ethics

Teacher notes

1. Explain that learners will now apply the learned structures and vocabulary in a more creative context.
2. Instruct them to imagine they are drafting a brief for their company's executive board, outlining a significant ethical challenge related to AI in customer interaction.
3. Emphasize the need to use at least one example of 'Prepositional Noun Phrase Fronting' (e.g., 'Under no circumstances...') and integrate several of the ethical AI phrases (e.g., addressing algorithmic bias, upholding data privacy).
4. Encourage them to maintain a C2 professional and diplomatic register.
5. Collect the drafts for review or facilitate small group discussions where learners present their briefings.
6. Sample Answer: 'Good morning, Board. Out of growing concerns for customer data misuse, arose our urgent need to re-evaluate current AI protocols. Under no circumstances can we afford to compromise user trust. Our new strategy prioritizes upholding data privacy and robustly addressing algorithmic bias across all customer-facing AI. We must also continue fostering responsible innovation while mitigating potential ethical implications.'

Student handout

Preparing for a Boardroom Discussion on AI

Imagine you are preparing a short executive briefing for your company's board of directors. Your task is to outline a critical ethical challenge related to the deployment of AI in customer interfaces and propose a high-level solution or commitment.

Your briefing should:
1. Identify a specific ethical concern (e.g., related to transparency, empathy gap, data sovereignty, algorithmic bias).
2. Express your company's commitment or a necessary action using at least one instance of a fronted prepositional/negative phrase with inverted word order (e.g., 'Under no circumstances...', 'Out of a commitment...').
3. Incorporate at least two other ethical AI terms from our lesson (e.g., ethical implications, fostering responsible innovation, upholding data privacy, escalation protocols).
4. Maintain a professional, diplomatic, and authoritative tone suitable for a C2 executive audience.

Aim for a short paragraph (3-5 sentences).
