Precision in Argumentation

In high-level communication, sometimes we don't ask questions to get new information, but to make an undeniable point. This is called Rhetorical Question Packaging for Advanced Strategic Logic.

Its purpose is to use a question format to highlight a fundamental truth, a serious contradiction, or a systemic vulnerability in formal reports and discussions.

Key Forms:
* Affirmative: An interrogative frame around an undeniable truth.
Example: _'How can a project succeed if its fundamental assumptions are flawed?'_
* Negative: Questioning a widely accepted negative truth to emphasize it.
Example: _'Is it not true that a lack of clear process undermines team efficiency?'_

Consider the examples below and refine them using these structures. Your goal is to make a definitive, strategic statement through the power of a well-formed question.
