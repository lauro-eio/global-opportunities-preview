  • A.symptomatic repair

  • C.root cause analysis ✓

3. q03grammar · class 1

  • A.Diagnosing system malfunctions through deductive reasoning is a skill critical for our technicians. ✓
  • B.To diagnose system malfunctions by deductive reasoning is a skill critical for our technicians.
  • C.Diagnose system malfunctions with deductive reasoning is a skill critical for our technicians.
  • D.The diagnosing of system malfunctions through deductive reasoning is a skill critical for our technicians.
ExplanationOption A uses a gerund phrase ('Diagnosing system malfunctions through deductive reasoning') as the subject of the sentence, which is grammatically correct and professional. Option B uses an infinitive phrase, which is less common for a nominal subject in this context. Option C is grammatically incorrect as 'Diagnose' is a verb in the imperative form, not a noun subject. Option D uses 'The diagnosing of', which is grammatically permissible but often considered less concise and direct than the simple gerund subject.
