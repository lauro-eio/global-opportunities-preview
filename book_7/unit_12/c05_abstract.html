Precision in Professional Communication

The phrases you encountered in the memo are powerful tools for communicating complex ideas with professionalism and precision. They are used to:

* Clearly explain the deeper reasons behind decisions.
* Describe important long-term goals and why they are necessary.
* Identify the central problems in a situation.
* Show how current actions fit into larger company objectives.

Listen carefully to your teacher for the correct pronunciation, focusing on emphasizing the key words in each phrase to convey authority and precision.

Key Phrases for Strategic Communication:

T2

Test 2: Ethical Case Study Discussion

Teacher notes

1. Divide learners into small groups (3-4 people).
2. Present the ethical dilemma case study. Ensure learners understand the core conflict.
3. Instruct them to discuss the situation, considering the questions provided.
4. Emphasize that they should use the target lexical phrases learned in the previous stage to articulate their reasoning, proposals, and considerations.
5. Circulate among groups, observing and listening for accurate and appropriate use of the target lexis. Note down examples of good usage and areas for improvement (e.g., mispronounced words, awkward phrasing, incorrect register).
6. Do not intervene unless absolutely necessary for comprehension. This is a fluency practice stage.
7. After the discussion, bring the class together. Ask each group to briefly present their key discussion points, encouraging them to use the target language. Provide constructive feedback on language use, focusing on accuracy, appropriacy, and pronunciation of the target phrases.

Model Response / Key Points (for teacher reference):
* To approach this, we first need to unpack the underlying logic of both the company's disciplinary policy and the ethical principles of support and fairness.
* We're conceptualizing the strategic imperative of maintaining team productivity while also fostering a supportive work environment.
* The core challenge here is balancing adherence to policy with compassion for a colleague's potential personal struggles.
* Our proposed solution must ultimately align with our broader vision of a high-performing yet empathetic workplace. Perhaps a confidential conversation to understand the personal issues, followed by a plan that supports the colleague while ensuring team responsibilities are met.

Student handout

Resolving Workplace Dilemmas: A Collaborative Approach

Work in small groups. Read the following workplace dilemma and discuss how you would approach it. Focus on using the professional phrases we have just learned to explain your reasoning and proposed solutions.

The Dilemma: The Struggling Colleague

Your colleague, an essential member of your team, has been consistently missing deadlines over the past month. This is causing significant delays for other team members and impacting overall project timelines. You suspect they might be dealing with personal issues outside of work, but company policy clearly states that repeated missed deadlines will lead to formal disciplinary action, potentially impacting their employment.

Discussion Questions:
1. How would you begin to analyze this situation, considering both company policy and ethical obligations to a colleague?
2. What are the key strategic considerations for the team and the company in the short and long term?
3. What is the main problem or conflict that needs to be resolved here?
4. How can any proposed solution reflect the larger values and goals of the organization?
5. What steps would you take, using our new phrases to explain your decision-making process?
