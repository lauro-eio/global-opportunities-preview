Lead-In: Workplace Well-being and Professional Support

Teacher notes

1. Begin by displaying the lead-in question or writing it on the board.
2. Divide students into pairs or small groups for discussion.
3. Monitor discussions, noting any emerging language or common misunderstandings related to professional boundaries.
4. Facilitate a brief whole-class feedback session to capture initial thoughts and set the context for the lesson.
5. Emphasize the importance of professional boundaries and the nuance of offering support without overstepping.

Supporting Colleagues: Ethical Considerations

We all experience periods of difficulty. It's natural to want to support a colleague who appears to be struggling. However, in a professional setting, there are ethical obligations and boundaries to consider.

Discuss the following questions with a partner:
1. What signs might indicate a colleague is struggling with their mental well-being at work?
2. What ethical obligations do colleagues and employers have to support an employee who is clearly struggling?
3. What are some potential risks or pitfalls if you try to help a colleague without maintaining appropriate professional boundaries?

Professional Boundaries: What Would You Say?

Read the internal memo from HR regarding employee well-being. Following the memo, you'll find scenarios where a manager needs to communicate about a colleague's well-being. Choose the most appropriate and professional phrasing for each situation, bearing in mind the guidelines from the memo.

Materials

Internal Memo: Employee Well-being Support

Subject: Important Update: Enhancing Employee Well-being Support

Dear Team Leaders,

This memo serves to reinforce our company's commitment to fostering a culture of well-being across all departments. We recognize that occasional personal difficulties can impact an individual's professional life. To support our employees effectively and ethically, we have established clear guidelines.

It is imperative that all managers and team leaders adhere to wellness protocols by directing colleagues who express or exhibit signs of struggling towards our official support channels, specifically the Employee Assistance Program (EAP). The EAP provides confidential, professional support and advice.

We kindly remind you to maintain a healthy work-life balance yourself and to encourage your teams to do the same. While compassion is vital, please refrain from offering personal advice, making diagnoses, or engaging in discussions that could overstep professional boundaries. Our focus must remain on providing structured, discreet, and appropriate support that respects privacy and professional dignity. Your active role in prioritizing mental well-being in your teams, through appropriate channels, is highly valued.

Best regards,
HR Department

Tasks

A colleague has seemed withdrawn and less productive. As their team leader, you decide to approach them discreetly. Which opening line is most professional and appropriate?

Choose the most professional opening line:

  • Are you feeling stressed out? You look really down.
  • I've noticed a change in your work recently. Is everything okay?
  • I've observed some changes in your recent work output, and I wanted to check in to see if there's anything I can support you with professionally.
  • You really need to get your act together; your mood is affecting the team.

A team member confides that they are feeling overwhelmed by personal issues. You want to suggest professional help without being intrusive. Complete the sentence below with the most appropriate phrase.

I understand you're going through a challenging time. Remember, the company offers confidential support through the Employee Assistance Program. They can help with __________ or general guidance. Would you like me to provide you with the details?

After a challenging project, you want to remind your team about the importance of recovery and personal time. Which statement best reinforces the idea of prioritizing mental well-being?

Choose the best statement:

  • Don't forget to take a break and chill out this weekend.
  • It's crucial that we all consider maintaining a healthy work-life balance to avoid burnout.
  • You guys worked hard, now go have some fun.
  • Make sure you come back refreshed for the next big project.

Teacher notes

1. Review the answers from T1, highlighting the phrases students struggled with.
2. Introduce the target exponents one by one from the column_banks.
3. For each exponent:
a. Elicit or explain the meaning and specific register (C2 professional/discreet).
b. Discuss its nuance and when it is appropriate to use, contrasting it with less formal alternatives.
c. Model pronunciation, drawing attention to stress on key content words and falling intonation for statements (phonological_focus). Use drilling if necessary.
d. Use Concept Checking Questions (CCQs) to ensure understanding (e.g., 'Can you use this with a close friend?' 'Does this sound casual or formal?' 'What's the main idea here?').
4. Guide students through the matching task to consolidate understanding of meaning and appropriacy.

Mastering Professional Language

Now, let's look at the specific phrases used in professional workplace conversations about health and well-being. Pay attention to their precise meaning, the situations in which they are appropriate, and how they sound when spoken.

Key Language for Workplace Health & Wellness Conversations

Materials

Column banks
Professional PhrasesMeaning & Nuance
maintaining a healthy work-life balanceEnsuring that the demands of work do not negatively impact personal life, promoting adequate rest and personal time. (Emphasizes long-term sustainability)
prioritizing mental well-beingActively placing importance on one's psychological and emotional health, often through self-care and seeking support. (Highlights proactive self-care)
adhering to wellness protocolsStrictly following established procedures and guidelines related to health, safety, and employee support within the organization. (Stresses compliance and formal procedures)
seeking professional medical adviceConsulting qualified healthcare professionals for diagnosis, treatment, or guidance regarding health issues. (Refers to formal, expert help, not self-diagnosis)
fostering a culture of well-beingCreating an environment where employees feel supported, valued, and encouraged to care for their overall health. (Focuses on collective, supportive workplace ethos)
Tasks

Match the professional phrase with the most appropriate context or situation where it would be used.

Match the phrases to their appropriate context.

  • maintaining a healthy work-life balance — Encouraging employees not to work excessive hours during busy periods.
  • prioritizing mental well-being — Reminding staff to take regular breaks and utilize stress reduction resources.
  • adhering to wellness protocols — Ensuring all managers follow HR's guidelines on discussing employee health.
  • seeking professional medical advice — Suggesting a colleague consults a doctor for persistent fatigue.
  • fostering a culture of well-being — Implementing new company-wide initiatives to support employee health.
T2

Production Test: Applying Professional Support Language

Teacher notes

1. Divide students into pairs, assigning 'Team Leader' and 'Alex' roles.
2. Review the scenario and task requirements, especially the need to use at least three target phrases.
3. Give students a few minutes to plan their approach and consider how they might integrate the target language.
4. Have students role-play the conversation. Circulate and monitor, noting effective use of target language, appropriate register, and areas for improvement in discretion or boundary maintenance.
5. After the role-play, facilitate a whole-class feedback session. Ask pairs to share how they started the conversation, what challenges they faced, and which phrases they found most useful.
6. Provide targeted feedback on language use, register, and pragmatic appropriacy. Model improved phrasing where necessary. Highlight examples of successful communication that demonstrate both professional support and boundary respect.

Model Response Considerations (for Team Leader):
* 'Alex, I've noticed a slight change in your engagement with recent projects, and I wanted to check in. Is everything alright? We value your contribution, and I want to ensure you have the support you need.'
* 'Remember, the company is committed to fostering a culture of well-being, and we have resources like the EAP available if personal matters are impacting your work.'
* 'It's important that we all focus on maintaining a healthy work-life balance, and I want to ensure you're able to do that.'
* 'If there's anything professionally I can do, or if you feel you need to discuss options for prioritizing mental well-being, please let me know.'
* 'We must always adhere to wellness protocols, which means I can't offer personal advice, but I can point you towards professional support.'

Ensure students understand that the goal is not to 'fix' Alex, but to offer professional support and point to appropriate resources while respecting boundaries.

Student handout

Navigating Delicate Conversations

You will now practice using the professional language from this lesson in a realistic workplace scenario. Work in pairs.

Scenario: You are a team leader. You've noticed one of your long-term team members, Alex, has been quieter than usual for the past few weeks. Their work quality has noticeably slipped on two recent projects, and they seem to be withdrawing from team interactions.

Your task: Engage in a conversation with Alex. Your goal is to:
1. Express your concern professionally and empathetically, without making assumptions or overstepping boundaries.
2. Remind Alex of the company's support systems if appropriate.
3. Use at least three of the target phrases we discussed (e.g., maintaining a healthy work-life balance, prioritizing mental well-being, adhering to wellness protocols, seeking professional medical advice, fostering a culture of well-being).

Alex's role: You are feeling overwhelmed by some personal issues, but you're not ready to share all the details. You appreciate your team leader's concern but are wary of revealing too much at work. You might initially deflect or minimize the issues.

Start the conversation. Be prepared to adapt your language based on Alex's responses.
