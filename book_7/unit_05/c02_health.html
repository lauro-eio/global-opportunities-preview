Lead-In: Ethical Health Data & Workplace Incentives

Teacher notes

1. Begin by displaying the lead-in brief on the board or projector.
2. Ask students to read the scenario individually.
3. Facilitate a whole-class discussion using the provided prompts, ensuring all students have a chance to contribute.
4. Encourage students to consider the perspectives of both employees and employers.

Your Health, Your Data, Your Choice?

Imagine your company offers a substantial bonus for employees who voluntarily share their health tracking data (e.g., fitness levels, sleep patterns, stress indicators) as part of a new 'Wellness Incentive Program'.

Discuss the following questions with a partner or in a small group:

1. What are the potential ethical implications of this policy for individual employees?
2. How might such a program affect workplace culture and trust?
3. What responsibilities do employers have regarding data privacy and confidentiality agreements when implementing such initiatives?
4. What long-term impact could this have on employee rights and potential discrimination in the workplace?

First Look: The 'Proactive Wellness' Memo

Read the internal memo from 'Aether Corp.' below. Don't worry about every detail for now, just try to understand the main purpose of the communication. Then, choose the best answer to the question.

Materials

Internal Memo: Launch of the Proactive Wellness Initiative (PWI)

DATE: October 26, 2023
TO: All Aether Corp. Employees
FROM: Human Resources Department
SUBJECT: Launching the 'Proactive Wellness Initiative' – Enhancing Our Collective Well-being

Dear Valued Team Members,

Aether Corp. is proud to announce the launch of our new 'Proactive Wellness Initiative' (PWI), effective January 1, 2024. This initiative underscores our unwavering commitment to fostering a culture of well-being and prioritizing mental well-being across our organization. We believe that a healthy, engaged workforce is fundamental to our shared success and productivity.

The PWI is designed to support you in maintaining a healthy work-life balance and empower you with tools to manage your personal health. We are offering a significant, voluntary wellness incentive – a bonus of up to 15% of your quarterly salary – for employees who opt to participate and share anonymized health metrics from approved wearables and health tracking applications. Participation requires that employees adhere to the program’s data-sharing protocols. It is crucial that all participating employees understand the terms of engagement.

To ensure transparency and protect individual data privacy, the data collected will be aggregated and anonymized by a third-party, certified health analytics firm. Our internal policies explicitly state that it is mandatory that employee-specific health data remain strictly confidential and not be used for performance evaluations or disciplinary actions. We are committed to upholding the highest standards of confidentiality agreements.

We recognize that concerns regarding employee rights and the ethical dimensions of health data collection are paramount. To address these, we strongly recommend that employees who are considering participation review the detailed PWI Policy Document available on the HR portal. This document outlines the safeguards in place to prevent discrimination and ensure equitable treatment. Additionally, we encourage you to seek professional medical advice if you have specific health concerns related to your participation.

We anticipate that this initiative will lead to a healthier, more vibrant Aether Corp. community. Your well-being is our priority.

FP

Freer Practice: Discussing Workplace Health Initiatives

Teacher notes

1. Divide students into small groups or pairs.
2. Present the scenario and discussion prompts.
3. Instruct students to use the analytical subjunctive (where appropriate for policy statements) and the professional health phrases learned in the lesson.
4. Circulate and monitor, noting good usage and areas for improvement.
5. Facilitate a brief whole-class feedback session, asking groups to share key points from their discussions.
Sample answers/points for discussion:
* Analytical Subjunctive: 'It is critical that employee consent be fully informed.', 'We recommend that data collection be strictly limited to anonymized aggregates.'
* Pragmatic Phrases: Emphasize fostering a culture of well-being through non-intrusive activities. Encourage maintaining a healthy work-life balance by offering flexible schedules. Highlight prioritizing mental well-being with confidential support resources. Stress the importance of adhering to wellness protocols that respect data privacy.
* Ethical considerations: Informed consent, anonymization, non-discrimination, clear benefits vs. risks, voluntary participation without subtle coercion.

Designing Your Own Ethical Wellness Program

Imagine your company wants to launch a truly ethical and effective 'Well-being at Work' program, but without offering monetary incentives that could coerce employees into sharing private data. They've asked your team to propose how such a program might function.

In your groups, discuss and outline key principles for this program. Try to use the formal subjunctive structures where you're stating mandates or recommendations, and integrate the professional health-related phrases we practiced earlier.

Consider:

1. What would be the core objectives of this program?
2. What steps would be taken to guarantee data privacy and protect employee rights?
3. How would the program encourage maintaining a healthy work-life balance and prioritizing mental well-being without intrusive monitoring?
4. What kind of activities or resources would be offered to foster a culture of well-being?
5. How would you communicate these principles to ensure trust and avoid any perception of discrimination?

Present your key proposals to the class, making sure your language is clear, professional, and reflects C2 discourse.
