TCH

Teach: Nuance, Register, and Pronunciation of Strategic Lexis

Teacher notes

1. Go over the answers to T1, eliciting why certain options are better than others, focusing on formality and nuance.
2. Introduce the target exponents: 'upskill proactively', 'tailor learning pathways', 'leverage professional development opportunities', 'align skill sets with industry demands', 'foster a growth mindset'.
3. Use CCQs to check understanding of meaning (e.g., 'What does it mean to 'upskill proactively'? Is it reactive or planned?').
4. Explain the register and appropriacy: these are formal, strategic phrases suitable for professional discussions, reports, and memos. Contrast with simpler, more informal equivalents.
5. Focus on phonology: Model pronunciation, highlighting word stress and intonation. For 'upskill proactively', emphasize stress on 'up' and 'pro'. For 'tailor learning pathways', stress 'tail' and 'path'. For 'leverage professional development opportunities', stress 'lev', 'fess', 'vel', 'tun'. For 'align skill sets with industry demands', stress 'lign', 'skill', 'dem'. For 'foster a growth mindset', stress 'fos', 'growth', 'mind'. Practice choral and individual drilling.
6. Use the matching task to reinforce meaning and the short answer task to practice using the language in context, focusing on appropriate register.
7. Provide further example sentences if needed to solidify understanding of context.

Mastering Professional Language for Upskilling

The phrases from the memo are highly valued in professional settings because they convey a strategic and proactive approach to career development. Let's look at their precise meanings, how and when to use them, and how to pronounce them clearly.

Meaning and Usage:

* Upskill proactively: To take initiative to improve your skills before they are absolutely required, anticipating future needs.
* Tailor learning pathways: To design individual courses of study or training plans that specifically meet your unique goals and needs.
* Leverage professional development opportunities: To make full and effective use of available training, courses, or resources for career growth.
* Align skill sets with industry demands: To ensure your abilities and qualifications are consistent with what the current job market or specific industry requires.
* Fostering a growth mindset: Cultivating an attitude that views challenges as opportunities for learning and development, rather than fixed limitations.

These expressions are more formal and strategic than simpler alternatives, suitable for professional communication, planning, and evaluation in the workplace.

T2

Test 2: Strategic Professional Development Discussion

Teacher notes

1. Divide students into pairs or small groups.
2. Present the role-play scenario and discussion questions.
3. Instruct students to use the newly acquired strategic phrases naturally and appropriately within the conversation.
4. Circulate and monitor, noting how accurately and appropriately students use the target language. Listen for register and phonology.
5. Provide delayed, constructive feedback on language use and register after the activity.
6. Model a brief example response for the prompt, demonstrating natural use of 2-3 target exponents (e.g., 'To prepare for new responsibilities, I'm planning to upskill proactively in project management. I'll also look to leverage professional development opportunities provided by the company, perhaps a certification, to really align my skill set with industry demands for leadership roles.')

Planning Your Professional Horizon

You are discussing your career aspirations and development plans with a colleague or a mentor. You want to articulate your strategies for professional growth clearly and professionally.

In pairs or small groups, discuss the following:
1. What specific areas are you looking to develop in the next 12-18 months, and how do you plan to upskill proactively in these areas?
2. How will you tailor learning pathways to ensure they are relevant to both your personal career goals and the needs of our organization?
3. Which professional development opportunities are you planning to leverage, either internal or external?
4. How are you ensuring your current skill sets align with industry demands and future trends?
5. What steps will you take to foster a growth mindset in yourself and perhaps even in your team?

Remember to use the strategic professional development phrases we've learned in your discussion.
