L-IN

Lead-In: Setting the Scene for Sustainable Workplaces

Teacher notes

1. Introduce the topic of workplace sustainability, linking to the company's goal to reduce environmental footprint.
2. Ask students to discuss the lead-in questions in pairs or small groups.
3. Encourage them to think about practical, actionable steps relevant to their own workplaces and home lives.
4. Facilitate a brief whole-class feedback session, noting key ideas on the board.

Student handout

Greener Work, Better Life

Our company wants to significantly reduce its environmental footprint across all its global offices. How can we make our workplaces and our home lives more sustainable?

Discuss with a partner:

1. What eco-friendly practices do you already see in your workplace, or in other companies you know?
2. What practical steps could your company introduce to reduce waste or optimize resources?
3. How could the company encourage employees to adopt more sustainable habits at home (e.g., eco-friendly commuting, responsible consumption)?
4. What are the biggest challenges to implementing green initiatives in a busy workplace?

Reading a Company-Wide Green Memo

Read the internal memo from 'Global Solutions Ltd.' about their new green initiatives. Pay attention to how they introduce the changes and encourage employee participation.

Materials

Internal Memo: Enhancing Our Commitment to Sustainability

Subject: Enhancing Our Commitment to Corporate Sustainability

Dear Team,

At Global Solutions Ltd., we are committed to reducing our environmental footprint across all our offices. We believe that by working together, we can genuinely contribute to a more sustainable future.

Effective next month, we will be introducing several new green initiatives aimed at resource optimization and waste reduction strategies. These include:

1. Digital-First Policy: Aiming for significantly less printing. Please use digital documents whenever possible and avoid unnecessary printouts.
2. Smart Recycling Bins: New bins will be installed in all common areas for easy separation of paper, plastic, and organic waste.
3. Energy-Saving Measures: Lights will automatically switch off in unoccupied rooms, and we encourage everyone to power down computers and monitors at the end of the day.

Furthermore, we are keen to support your personal eco-friendly commuting efforts. We will be launching a cycle-to-work scheme next quarter and exploring options for public transport subsidies. We also encourage responsible consumption in the office kitchen by using reusable cups and reducing single-use plastics.

Your participation is crucial for the success of these initiatives. We will be holding a brief informational session next week to provide more details and answer any questions. Let's make a positive impact together.

Sincerely,
The Global Solutions Ltd. Sustainability Team

Tasks

What is the main purpose of this memo?

  • To demand immediate compliance with new rules.
  • To inform about new sustainability initiatives and encourage employee participation.
  • To complain about current wasteful practices in the office.

Which of these is NOT a new initiative explicitly mentioned for the office environment?

  • A 'Digital-First Policy' to reduce printing.
  • Providing free electric scooters for all employees.
  • Installing 'Smart Recycling Bins' for waste separation.

How does the memo encourage eco-friendly commuting?

  • By launching a cycle-to-work scheme and exploring public transport subsidies.
  • By banning personal cars from the company parking lot.
  • By offering company-provided carpool services with fixed routes.

Student handout

Crafting Your Green Message

Imagine your company is introducing its own set of green initiatives. Before you write your announcement, let's plan!

Brainstorm: Think about your own workplace or a hypothetical one. What specific actions could be taken to promote corporate sustainability, resource optimization, and waste reduction strategies? Consider both actions the company takes and habits employees can adopt.

Outline: What are the key points you need to include in your announcement? Think about:
* Purpose: Why is this important? (e.g., reduce environmental footprint)
* Actions: What specific initiatives are being introduced? (e.g., smart recycling, eco-friendly commuting incentives)
* Benefits: How will this help the company and employees?
* Call to Action: How can employees get involved? (e.g., attend a briefing, adopt responsible consumption)
* Next Steps: Any follow-up meetings or resources?

FP

Free Practice: Drafting a Sustainability Memo

Teacher notes

1. Remind students of the planning they did in the previous stage and the model text from MOD.
2. Instruct students to write their memo/email based on the brief, using the model text and useful phrases as a guide.
3. Circulate and monitor, offering support as needed. Remind them to focus on clarity, professional tone, and including actionable steps.
4. Model Answer for Teacher:

Subject: Important Update: Launching Our New Green Initiative

Dear Team,

Building on our commitment to corporate sustainability, we are excited to announce new initiatives designed to significantly reduce our environmental impact and foster a greener working environment.

Effective next month, we are implementing comprehensive waste reduction strategies by introducing composting bins in all kitchen areas and partnering with a local provider for electronics recycling. Furthermore, we are promoting resource optimization through smart heating and cooling systems installed across our offices to minimize energy consumption.

We also want to encourage eco-friendly commuting by expanding our secure bike storage facilities and launching a new employee carpooling program. At home, we urge everyone to consider responsible consumption practices and utilize the educational resources we'll share on our intranet next week.

Your active participation is vital for the success of these programs. Please attend our online briefing on [Date] to learn more about how you can contribute.

Let's work together to build a truly sustainable workplace.

Best regards,
The Leadership Team

Student handout

Your Company's Green Message

Now, write an internal memo or email to all employees at your company, introducing new green initiatives. Follow the outline you prepared earlier.

Audience: All employees (colleagues).
Purpose: To inform about new sustainability initiatives, explain their importance, and encourage participation in both the workplace and employees' home lives.
Format: Internal memo or email.
Approximate Length: 150-250 words.

Remember to:
* Start with a clear purpose for the memo.
* Detail at least three specific corporate sustainability initiatives (e.g., resource optimization, waste reduction strategies, eco-friendly commuting).
* Use clear and professional language, drawing on the phrases from the previous stage.
* Include a call to action or next steps for employees.

FBDC

Feedback and Correction: Reviewing Sustainability Memos

Teacher notes

1. Organize students into pairs or small groups for peer feedback.
2. Instruct them to read each other's memos and provide feedback based on the prompts provided in student_content.
3. Circulate and monitor, addressing any issues and guiding students in their feedback.
4. Collect the memos for teacher feedback focusing on content, organization, clarity, and language accuracy related to the target lexis.
5. Highlight common errors or particularly effective uses of language in a subsequent session.

Student handout

Improving Our Green Messages

Exchange your memo with a partner. Read their memo and give constructive feedback based on the following points:

Content & Clarity:
1. Is the purpose of the memo clear from the beginning?
2. Are the green initiatives clearly explained and easy to understand?
3. Are there practical, actionable steps for both the workplace and potentially home life?
4. Does the overall message sound professional and encouraging?

Language & Style:
5. Are the lexical tags (e.g., corporate sustainability, resource optimization, waste reduction strategies, eco-friendly commuting, responsible consumption, green initiatives) used appropriately and effectively?
6. Is the tone appropriate for an internal company communication?
7. Are there any sentences that are unclear or could be phrased more effectively?

After discussing, revise your own memo based on the feedback.
