1. q01lexis · class 1
With the increasing prevalence of automation, the company is investing heavily in employee __________ programs to ensure the workforce remains relevant.
  • A.upskilling ✓

2. q02comprehension · class 1
The latest report indicates that while automation is displacing some entry-level positions, it is simultaneously creating new highly specialized 'analyst' and 'maintenance' job roles. What is the primary implication for workforce planning?
  • A.The overall number of job roles will drastically decrease across the board.
