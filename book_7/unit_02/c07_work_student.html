Clarifying Formal Demands and Recommendations

When making formal recommendations, demands, or suggestions that are urgent or strategic, especially in a professional context, we often use a specific grammatical structure known as the subjunctive.

Look at these examples and rules:

Form:

* Subject + demand/suggest/recommend/insist + that + Subject + bare infinitive verb
* Negative: Subject + demand/suggest/recommend/insist + that + Subject + not + bare infinitive verb

Notice that the verb after 'that' does not change, even for third-person singular subjects (he/she/it). It always stays in its base form.

Meaning:
This structure emphasizes the importance or urgency of a recommendation, demand, or suggestion, often for high-stakes decisions, strategic planning, or policy implementation within an organization.
