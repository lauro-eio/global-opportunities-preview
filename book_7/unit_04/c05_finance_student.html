Your Workplace & Economic Shifts

Imagine the central bank has just announced an unexpected interest rate increase. This decision, while made at a high level, can have real and tangible effects on your workplace and the wider local economy.

Discuss in pairs or small groups:
1. How might this change affect your current job or a past role you've held? Consider areas like customer spending, company loans for expansion, pricing of goods/services, or even staffing decisions.
2. What might your manager or company leadership be discussing internally about these potential impacts?
3. How would you formally communicate these potential changes to colleagues, a team, or even clients? What kind of language would you use?

Understanding Workplace Memos

Read the internal memo below from a company's finance department. It discusses the recent interest rate increase and its potential impact. Pay attention to the tone and word choices.

Then, complete the tasks that follow, choosing the most appropriate options for a professional workplace communication.

Materials

Internal Memo: Post-Interest Rate Hike Briefing

Subject: Urgent Update: Economic Shifts & Company Preparedness

Team,

Following the central bank's unexpected interest rate increase, our finance department has been busy getting their heads around the potential implications for our operations. While it's a bit of a headache for everyone, we've started to evaluate our projected growth rates for the coming quarter.

We anticipate some market volatility trends in consumer spending and raw material costs. This isn't exactly a great situation, and it points to an adverse economic outlook if we don't quickly sort out potential risks now. We'll be making some internal fiscal policy adjustments to our budget and spending plans. Expect further details soon.

Your understanding and cooperation are vital as we navigate these changes.

Regards,

Finance Department

Speaking Professionally about Finance

In professional environments, especially when discussing finance and economics, using the correct register is essential for clear, objective, and credible communication. Let's look at some key formal expressions for assessing economic outlooks and contrast them with more informal alternatives.

These phrases help you communicate complex financial ideas with precision and authority.

Reporting on Economic Impact

You are a team leader in your department (e.g., logistics, sales, operations). Your manager has asked you to prepare a brief internal update for your team, explaining the potential impact of the central bank's recent interest rate increase on your specific department's operations or future plans.

In pairs, take turns being the 'team leader' and 'team member'. The team leader should use as many of the formal expressions we've learned (e.g., projected growth rates, fiscal policy adjustments, mitigate potential risks, market volatility trends, adverse economic outlook) as possible to convey a clear, professional, and objective assessment of the situation.

Team members should listen carefully and ask clarifying questions using polite, professional language.

Think about how this economic shift might affect daily tasks, budgeting, customer interactions, or supplier relationships in your area.
