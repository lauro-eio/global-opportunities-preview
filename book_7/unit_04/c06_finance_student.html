The Risky Proposition: Your Initial Thoughts

Imagine your company has been approached with a highly profitable international deal. Your business partner, however, suggests a shortcut – a way to 'bend' certain financial regulations to maximize profits. This could significantly boost your company's revenue.

Discuss with a partner:
1. What are your immediate concerns when hearing about 'bending' international financial regulations?
2. What are the potential short-term benefits and long-term risks (both ethical and legal) for your company and its reputation?
3. What questions would you raise immediately with your business partner or internal legal team?
4. How important is regulatory compliance in the current global economic climate?

Internal Memo: Due Diligence on 'Horizon' Acquisition

To: Head of Compliance
From: Senior Financial Analyst
Date: 15 March 20XX
Subject: Preliminary Assessment – Proposed 'Horizon' Acquisition & Associated Risks

Dear [Head of Compliance’s Name],

This memo outlines my preliminary assessment of the proposed 'Horizon' acquisition, specifically regarding certain operational aspects that have raised significant concerns from a regulatory compliance standpoint.

While the financial projections for the 'Horizon' acquisition are undeniably attractive, initial discussions with our proposed partners have surfaced several areas requiring immediate and thorough due diligence. Specifically, the suggested mechanism for cross-border fund transfers, which purports to 'streamline' the process, appears to bypass standard protocols for anti-money laundering (AML) checks in two key jurisdictions. This raises a red flag regarding potential violations of international financial statutes and could expose our firm to considerable legal and reputational risks.

Furthermore, the current proposal lacks clear provisions for robust accountability mechanisms in its corporate governance structure, particularly concerning fund allocation post-acquisition. This omission could complicate future audits and compromise our commitment to corporate social responsibility (CSR). It suggests a potential disregard for transparent practices that could lead to unforeseen liabilities.

I recommend we immediately engage independent legal counsel specializing in international financial law to review the proposed transaction structure. Additionally, a comprehensive risk assessment, focusing specifically on AML and regulatory compliance for cross-border operations, is imperative. Proceeding without such scrutiny could jeopardize our standing and incur severe penalties.

I believe it is crucial that we ensure this deal aligns perfectly with our ethical guidelines and all applicable international financial regulations. I am available to discuss these points further and to coordinate any necessary investigative steps.

Sincerely,
[Senior Financial Analyst Name]

Your Memo: Advocating for Ethical Compliance

You are a senior manager in your company. Your business partner has just proposed a highly lucrative deal, but during a preliminary discussion, they mentioned a method for 'optimizing' cross-border transactions that you suspect might 'bend' international financial regulations. You are concerned about the ethical and legal implications.

Your Task: Write an internal memo to your Head of Compliance and CEO (or equivalent senior management) formally outlining your concerns about this proposed 'optimization' method.

Audience: Senior Management (Head of Compliance, CEO)
Purpose: To formally document your concerns, detail potential risks, and propose immediate, ethical steps forward to protect the company.
Length: Approximately 200-250 words.

Ensure your memo:
* Clearly states the purpose of the memo.
* Identifies the specific area of concern (without making accusations).
* Explains the potential risks (legal, reputational, financial).
* Uses appropriate formal language and target vocabulary (e.g., regulatory compliance, due diligence, anti-money laundering).
* Proposes a clear, professional course of action to address the concerns.

Strengthening Your Message: Peer Review

Now, you will exchange your memo with a partner and provide constructive feedback. Use the following questions to guide your review.

Part 1: Content and Clarity
1. Does the memo clearly state the writer's main concern about the proposed deal?
2. Are the potential risks (legal, reputational, financial) clearly identified and explained?
3. Does the memo propose concrete and professional steps to address these concerns?
4. Is the overall message logical and easy to follow?
5. Is the tone appropriate for senior management (professional, cautious, not overly emotional)?

Part 2: Language and Formality
1. Has the writer used appropriate formal language throughout the memo?
2. Are key terms like regulatory compliance, due diligence, anti-money laundering, and accountability mechanisms used correctly and effectively?
3. Are there any sentences that could be more concise or clear?
4. Check for any grammatical errors or awkward phrasing that might reduce the memo's professional impact.
