1. q01lexis · class 1
Our recent analysis highlights a critical geographic area where political instability severely impacts the flow of goods. This region has become a significant _______ for international shipping routes.
  • A.bottleneck
  • B.choke point ✓
  • C.dead end
  • D.pressure point
ExplanationA 'choke point' specifically refers to a narrow passage or area, such as a strait or canal, where traffic can be easily stopped or interrupted, making it a critical vulnerability in logistics and trade. 'Bottleneck' is a close synonym but 'choke point' is the more precise and common term in global trade lexicon for geographic vulnerabilities.
