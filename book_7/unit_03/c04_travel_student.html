Tasks

Match the words on the left with their definitions on the right.

Match the words with their definitions:

  • routing — The process of planning the path for data, vehicles, or goods.
  • network operations — The management and monitoring of a system of interconnected elements (e.g., transport routes, data flow).
  • updates — The latest information or new details about a situation.
  • delays — Situations where something is postponed or slowed down, causing it to be late.
  • re-route — To change the planned course or path of something.
  • congestion — A situation where there is too much traffic or activity, causing blockages and slowing things down.

Materials

Urgent Dispatch Briefing: Southern Bypass Incident

Good morning, dispatch team and all drivers on the Southern Bypass sector. This is Maria, your Lead Coordinator, with an urgent network operations update. We've just received confirmation of a major incident on the Southern Bypass, near Junction 17. A multi-vehicle accident has completely blocked all lanes, eastbound and westbound. This is causing severe congestion and will result in significant delays across all scheduled deliveries relying on that primary routing channel.

Our immediate priority is to minimize disruption. We are actively working on a plan to re-route all affected vehicles. Please listen carefully: effective immediately, all traffic originally scheduled to use the Southern Bypass through Junction 17 must re-route. For eastbound traffic, use the designated alternative via the A12 Northern Link, then reconnect at Junction 21. Westbound traffic, divert immediately via the B304, approaching from the south.

I repeat, this is a critical update due to an unforeseen incident. Do not attempt to use the Southern Bypass near Junction 17. Our mapping systems are being updated in real-time to reflect these new routing instructions. Supervisors will be contacting individual drivers shortly with precise revised schedules and any additional instructions. Your cooperation in executing these emergency re-route plans is paramount to maintaining our service levels. We will provide further updates as the situation evolves. Thank you.
