Materials

Memo: Strategic Workforce Readiness for Q3/Q4

To: All Department Heads
From: Eleanor Vance, Senior Operations Director
Date: 2024-05-15
Subject: Proactive Measures for Anticipated Operational Shifts

Team,

As we approach the second half of the fiscal year, it is imperative that we critically assess our human capital strategy. Proactive strategic foresight is crucial to navigating the evolving demands of our operational landscape. Our analysis indicates a potential seasonal surge in project volume, particularly within the logistics and client support divisions, commencing early Q3 and extending through Q4.

To mitigate foreseeable risks and ensure sustained operational excellence, we are launching several key initiatives. First, a comprehensive review of our current skill matrix is underway to identify potential gaps in critical areas. This will directly inform a new cross-training initiative, designed to enhance team versatility and resilience.

Furthermore, addressing potential workforce attrition and strengthening our talent pipeline remain top priorities. We will intensify our succession planning efforts, ensuring that key roles are continuously supported by a robust pool of qualified internal candidates.

Notwithstanding the inherent complexities of long-term economic forecasting, our commitment to proactive staffing solutions is unwavering. We must draw upon past insights to anticipate potential pitfalls and inform future strategies, thereby leverage prior learning to secure our competitive advantage. Your active participation in these initiatives is vital.

Thank you,

Eleanor Vance
Senior Operations Director

GD

Guided Discovery: Strategic Communication

Teacher notes

1. Direct learners to focus on specific sentences from the memo.
2. For Task 1, guide learners to identify the part of the sentence that introduces a challenge or concession, and then the part that states the company's main commitment despite that challenge. Emphasize how this structure maintains a professional and decisive tone.
3. For Task 2, ask learners to find phrases related to using past experience for future planning. Discuss the C2-level nuance of these phrases compared to simpler expressions. For example, 'draw upon past insights' is more formal than 'remember what happened before'.
4. Elicit the meaning and pragmatic function of these linguistic features – how they convey authority and certainty while acknowledging difficulties.

Tasks

Look at the sentence: 'Notwithstanding the inherent complexities of long-term economic forecasting, our commitment to proactive staffing solutions is unwavering.' What does the phrase 'Notwithstanding the inherent complexities...' do? And what is the main clause 'our commitment... is unwavering' affirming despite this?

1. What does 'Notwithstanding...' acknowledge? 2. What does the main clause affirm?

Match the phrases from the memo on the left with their meaning related to 'Leveraging Experience for Proactive Planning' on the right.

Match phrases to their function.

  • draw upon past insights — To use knowledge gained from previous situations.
  • anticipate potential pitfalls — To foresee and prepare for possible problems or challenges.
  • inform future strategies — To use current knowledge to shape plans for what comes next.
  • leverage prior learning — To effectively utilize previous knowledge or experience.
  • mitigate foreseeable risks — To reduce the severity or likelihood of predicted dangers.

FP

Freer Practice: Strategic Planning Application

Teacher notes

1. Explain that learners will now apply both the concessive structures and the 'Leveraging Experience' phrases in a more open context.
2. Present the scenario: a new cross-departmental project with identified challenges and the need to plan effectively.
3. Instruct learners to draft a short paragraph (2-3 sentences) or prepare a brief verbal statement outlining their strategy. They must use at least one concessive modifier (e.g., Notwithstanding...) and at least two phrases for leveraging experience (e.g., draw upon past insights, anticipate potential pitfalls).
4. Monitor and provide support as learners draft their responses.
5. Have learners share their statements in pairs or small groups, followed by a brief whole-class share. Emphasize the professional and diplomatic register.
Sample Answer: 'Notwithstanding the inherent challenges of integrating disparate systems, our team will draw upon past insights from similar implementations to anticipate potential pitfalls. This will allow us to inform future strategies effectively and ensure a smoother rollout.'

Student handout

Developing a Future Strategy

Imagine your department is about to embark on a new cross-departmental project. You have identified some potential challenges, but you also want to reassure stakeholders that the project will be successful due to careful planning and past experience.

Draft a short statement (2-3 sentences) for an internal briefing. In your statement, you must:
1. Use at least one rhetorical subordination post concessive modifier (e.g., 'Notwithstanding...', 'Albeit...').
2. Use at least two phrases related to 'Leveraging Experience for Proactive Planning' (e.g., 'draw upon past insights', 'anticipate potential pitfalls', 'inform future strategies', 'leverage prior learning', 'mitigate foreseeable risks').

Be prepared to share your statement with a partner or the class.
