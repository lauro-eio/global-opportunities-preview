L-IN

Lead-In: Payslip Puzzles

Teacher notes

1. Welcome learners and introduce the topic of payslips.
2. Ask learners to share any confusing lines or terms they've seen on a payslip (real or invented).
3. Facilitate a short discussion about what numbers are typically found on a payslip and what they represent.
4. Emphasize the importance of understanding these numbers for personal finance, rather than focusing on a full household budget.

Student handout

Understanding Your Payslip

Think about your own payslip or imagine one.

1. Is there any part of a payslip that you find confusing? For example, a deduction, or a line like 'Gross Pay'.
2. What numbers and information do you usually see on a payslip?
3. Why do you think it's important to understand your payslip?

Pre-Teach Vocabulary: Payslip Terms

Teacher notes

1. Introduce the key vocabulary terms related to payslips.
2. Ask learners if they already know any of the words and what they mean.
3. Instruct learners to complete the matching task individually or in pairs.
4. Elicit answers from the class and clarify the meaning of each term, using simple definitions and examples relevant to their work lives.

Student handout

Key Words for Your Payslip

We are going to learn some important words about payslips. Read the words below. Do you know what they mean? We will match them to their definitions.

Materials

Vocabulary
  • payslip
  • gross pay
  • net pay
  • deductions
  • tax
  • insurance
Tasks

Match each payslip term to its correct definition.

  • payslip — A document showing how much you have earned and how much has been taken out of your pay.
  • gross pay — The total amount of money you earn before any deductions are taken out.
  • net pay — The amount of money you receive after all deductions have been taken from your gross pay.
  • deductions — Amounts of money that are taken out of your gross pay, like tax or insurance.
  • tax — Money that you have to pay to the government from your income.
  • insurance — Money paid to a company for protection against loss, damage, or illness, often deducted from your pay.
GIST

Gist Reading: Maria's Payslip

Teacher notes

1. Introduce the reading text about Maria and her payslip.
2. Explain the task: Learners should read the text quickly to understand the main idea, without focusing on every detail.
3. Set a short time limit (e.g., 2-3 minutes) for reading.
4. Instruct learners to answer the multiple-choice question to check their general understanding.
5. Check the answer with the class and briefly discuss why it is the correct main idea.

Student handout

Understanding Maria's Payslip

Read the text quickly. Don't worry about understanding every single word. Just try to get the main idea of why Maria checks her payslip. Then answer the question below.

Materials

Maria's Monthly Payslip Check

Maria works in a local bakery. Every month, she makes sure to check her payslip carefully. This month, she noticed something that looked a little different.

Her payslip always starts with her gross pay. This is the total money she earned before anything is taken out. For Maria, this was £1800. After that, she looks at the deductions. These are the amounts of money that are removed from her gross pay. The biggest deduction is usually tax. Maria's tax deduction this month was £200.

She also has insurance deducted, which is £50, and a pension contribution of £70. All these amounts are subtracted from her gross pay. What is left is her net pay. This is the money that actually arrives in her bank account. This month, Maria's net pay was £1480.

Maria always checks that her net pay is correct and that the deductions make sense. If anything looks wrong or she doesn't understand a line, she knows she needs to ask her manager or the HR department. It’s important to understand where your money goes.

Tasks

What is the main reason Maria checks her payslip every month?

  • To calculate her household budget for the month.
  • To make sure her pay is correct and all her deductions are explained.
  • To compare her salary with her colleagues at the bakery.
DET

Detailed Reading: Maria's Payslip Details

Teacher notes

1. Instruct learners to read the text again, this time focusing on specific details.
2. Have learners complete the short-text questions individually or in pairs.
3. Check answers for the factual questions with the class.
4. Divide learners into small groups or pairs for discussion based on the open prompts in student_content.
5. Monitor discussions, offering support with vocabulary if needed.
6. Elicit ideas from the groups for the discussion questions.
* Model Answers for discussion:
1. She checks to make sure she has been paid the correct amount and that all money taken out (deductions) is correct and understood.
2. If she didn't check, she might receive the wrong amount of pay, or money might be taken out incorrectly without her knowing.
3. She can ask her manager or the Human Resources (HR) department at her workplace.

FBDC

Follow-up: Your Payslip, Your Rights

Teacher notes

1. Facilitate a class discussion based on the prompts in student_content.
2. Encourage learners to reflect on their own understanding of payslips after the lesson.
3. Elicit and summarize two key learnings about payslips from the class.
4. Discuss practical steps and who to contact if a mistake is found on a payslip, reinforcing the practical utility of the lesson.
5. Provide brief, encouraging feedback on language use during the discussions, focusing on communication and fluency rather than specific errors.
