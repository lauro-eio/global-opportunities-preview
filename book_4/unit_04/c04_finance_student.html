When Your Pay Changes

Imagine you are expecting your salary on a specific day, but something changes. How do you usually find out if your pay is late, early, or different from what you expected?

1. Who usually tells you (HR, your supervisor, the bank)?
2. How do they tell you (email, app notification, phone call)?
3. How quickly do you need to know about these changes?

Key Words for Your Pay

Here are some words you will hear in today's listening. Match them with their correct meaning.

Materials

Vocabulary
  • payroll
  • pay day
  • bank transfer
  • delay
  • update
  • processing
Tasks

Match each term to its definition.

  • payroll — The list of employees and their salaries.
  • pay day — The specific day when salaries are usually paid.
  • bank transfer — Moving money electronically from one bank account to another.
  • delay — A period of time when something is held up or happens later than planned.
  • update — New information about a situation.
  • processing — The action of dealing with information or a request.

Materials

Important Payroll Update

Good morning, team. This is Maria from HR with an important payroll update. I'm calling to inform you about a slight delay with this month's pay day. Due to an unexpected technical issue with our bank transfer system, some salaries are taking a little longer to process than usual. We understand this might cause inconvenience, and we sincerely apologize. The team is actively working with the bank to resolve this. We expect all payments to be fully processed and visible in your accounts by tomorrow morning at the latest. Please do not contact HR or your supervisors directly about this today, as we will send another update via email once the issue is completely resolved. Thank you for your patience and understanding.

Talking About Urgent Information

Discuss these questions with your partner or the class:

1. How would you feel if you received this update about your pay day?
2. Why is it important for employers to give clear information about payroll delays?
3. Have you ever experienced a delay with your bank transfer or pay? What happened?
4. What kind of update (e.g., email, app notification, phone call) would you prefer for urgent news about your pay?
