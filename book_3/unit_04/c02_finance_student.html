Quick Scan: The Problem

Read the urgent report about a delivery. Try to understand the main idea quickly.

Materials

Urgent: Delivery Check Report

Subject: Urgent Delivery Check – Order #87345

Hi Team,

Please see the report for yesterday's delivery. We received 50 boxes of safety gloves. The delivery note showed 60 boxes. Also, the invoice listed the unit price for bolts as £0.50, but our purchase order said £0.45. This means the total amount on the invoice was wrong. We sent an email to the supplier about these issues right away. We asked them to correct the invoice and re-send it. We did not sign the original delivery note due to the problems. We need to make sure everything is exact. Thanks, Operations Manager.
