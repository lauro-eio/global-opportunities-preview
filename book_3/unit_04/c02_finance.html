Lead-In: Checking a Delivery

Teacher notes

1. Greet learners and introduce the topic using the lead-in brief from the class reference.
2. Elicit ideas about checking deliveries at work.
3. Ask learners to discuss the questions in pairs.
4. Elicit some answers from the class, focusing on the importance of accuracy and avoiding mistakes with money or stock.

GIST

Gist Reading: Delivery Discrepancy

Teacher notes

1. Distribute the report to learners.
2. Explain that learners need to read quickly for the main idea, not for every detail.
3. Ask learners to complete the multiple-choice task individually.
4. Check answers as a class, confirming the main problem (discrepancy between documents and actual delivery).

GD

Guided Discovery: Past Simple & Invoice Terms

Teacher notes

1. Direct learners to focus on the verbs highlighted in bold in the report.
2. Ask questions to elicit the tense and meaning (e.g., 'Is this happening now?', 'Is it finished?', 'Do we know when it happened?'). Connect these to the concept check questions.
3. Introduce the term 'Past Simple' and guide learners to notice the form (verb+ed or irregular, 'did not' for negative).
4. For lexis, draw attention to the vocabulary items highlighted with double asterisks in the report.
5. Ask learners to complete the matching task for vocabulary, linking words to their definitions.
6. Clarify definitions and pronunciation as needed.

FP

Freer Practice: Workplace Checks

Teacher notes

1. Introduce the freer practice activity, explaining that learners will talk about their own experiences.
2. Give learners a few minutes to think about a time they checked something important at work and to plan what they will say.
3. Encourage them to use the Past Simple and some of the target vocabulary from the lesson.
4. Put learners in pairs to share their stories.
5. Monitor for correct use of the Past Simple and target vocabulary, noting down any common errors for delayed correction or whole-class feedback.
6. Ask a few pairs to share brief versions of their stories with the whole class.
